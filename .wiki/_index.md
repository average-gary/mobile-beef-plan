## Scope

See [config.md](config.md). In brief: what it takes to start and run a mobile beef slaughter
business around Winchester / Frederick County, VA, with the three regulatory models held side by
side. Every number carries its regulatory model and year.

## Key Findings

- **The model (chosen, round 3):** a custom-exempt mobile unit **kills on the owner's farm and later cuts and
  packages**, docked at a company-owned **hanging and aging cooler**. This is legal under a free VDACS Custom Permit
  (a "mobile slaughter and processing unit" is expressly allowed). Owning the cooler and the cutting removes the
  partner-cut-shop dependency that idled other mobile units.
- **Cutting is the ceiling.** About 8 man-hours per custom beef, so two people manage about 6–10 head/week (about 300–400/yr).
  Revenue is about $800–1,100/head (farm-call kill $100–150 + mileage + daily minimum; cut and pack $0.90–1.05/lb).
  Virginia butcher wage: $20.03/hr.
- **The cooler site is a processing site.** Frederick County RA lists slaughterhouses ("processing of meats") only as a
  **conditional use** and has no cold-storage use. Off the farm, a cooler with a cutting dock is by right **only in M2**
  (Industrial General); B2 commercial does not allow it (round 4). Get a written use determination first.
- **Wastewater is the hard part.** Blood and wash water are DEQ "industrial wastes" with no small-processor general permit.
  Capturing blood cuts wastewater strength about tenfold. Offal composting on the farm of kill is likely exempt (9VAC20-81-95 D.4), and
  Darling renders in Winchester (one renderer statewide).
- **Equipment:** the kill-and-cut mobile unit (Sentinel 53 ft) is from $300k; custom kill-only trailers $72k–$115k; a cooler for
  20–30 hanging beef is about 300–500 sq ft (CoolBot walk-in from $5,100; plant-standard about $300/sq ft).
- **Demand:** regional plants at 125–150% of capacity; the state plan records 18–24 month waits and backs
  custom-exempt capacity. Fixed USDA all-in is about $860–975 per 750 lb carcass, so the mobile service sells no wait, not price.
- **Virginia is not a CIS state.** Custom beef has no retail channel.
- **Money:** no grant pays a for-profit startup directly. Use Farm Credit plus VSBFA debt. **AFID Infrastructure fits the
  cooler** (through a locality; next deadline **Oct 30, 2026**). FSA operating loans are barred.

## Key Findings: options (round 4)

- **Five models compared** on the [Business Model Options](wiki/topics/business-model-options.md) hub: A kill-only + partner
  shop (superseded), B cooler-dock kill-and-cut (chosen), C storage cooler + cut on farm, D rental + storage, E rental + education.
- **The advise-vs-participate line** (Option E): every text found turns on who physically performs the slaughter or cutting [inferred],
  not on payment. Classroom and planning advice are very likely fine; hands-off coaching is a gray zone; any hands-on act makes
  the company a custom operator. Ask VDACS in writing; hold the free Custom Permit as a safe harbor.
- **No red-meat DIY rental precedent** in NMPAN's directory (c. 2012-2015); all rental programs it lists are poultry ($25-150/day). Virginia voids pre-injury
  personal-injury waivers (*Hiett*, 1992), so insurance carries rental and workshop risk.

## Key Findings: price tiers (round 5)

- **[Option F](wiki/options/option-f-tiered-offering.md)** combines the options into tiers on one unit and cooler: T1 Rent (D),
  T2 Coached (E, advise only), T3 Full service (B), plus add-ons (aging, storage, SRM, offal haul-off, mileage, supplies, delivery).
- **A tier is a legal configuration:** staff hands-on work on a T1/T2 job makes it custom-exempt with the company as operator.
  Personal-use carcass storage and on-site coaching need VDACS answers. No tier may cut at a B3 storage cooler.
- **Economics (calculator tier mix):** at default staffing the 50/30/20 mix lowers Y3 cash to $94,952 and lifts break-even with draw to
  311 head; with payroll matched to labor it gives $120,495 and 274 head. DIY tiers hit the unit calendar first [inferred].
- **Gore's Meats** (Frederick County incumbent) price list found: $100 kill + $1.05/lb (2026); a $105 kill-and-age tier in 2023.

## Open Questions

1. Frederick County use determination / CUP for the cooler and dock; Shenandoah, Clarke and Page comparison.
2. Sewer availability and FWSA discharge limits at candidate sites.
3. DEQ: D.4 for offal, and the agricultural exclusion for a mobile operator's blood and wash water.
4. Quotes: kill-and-cut unit (MPTS gooseneck, Sentinel 53 ft, Friesla) and cooler build; rail and trolley prices.
5. VDACS OMPS: does the Custom Permit cover the docked cut room and cooler; the Valley custom-permit roster.
6. Local producers' willingness to pay; Mid-Atlantic farm-kill prices (phone only).
7. Virginia insurance premiums (NCCI 2081, liability, spoilage, bailee; hands-on workshop GL; rental inland marine).
8. VDACS OMPS: what counts as "participating" for an adviser or instructor at a renter's kill; Handlers Permit for a cooler holding owner-held carcasses.
9. Zoning Administrator: does § 165-204.17 apply in M2; is a fee-based carcass cooler "Warehousing and Storage" (B3).
