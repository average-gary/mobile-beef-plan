# Option D: Rental Plus Storage

> **Status: candidate.** Explored 2026-10-03. Round 4 checked the rental, storage and insurance questions.
> Its education extension is [[option-e-rental-plus-education|Option E]] ([Option E](option-e-rental-plus-education.md)).
> All options: [[business-model-options|Business Model Options]] ([Business Model Options](../topics/business-model-options.md)). Not legal advice.

## How it works

1. The owner rents the mobile unit (or a towable kill trailer) and **does the kill and cut themselves**.
2. The company stores carcasses (hanging) and, optionally, frozen boxes in a storage-only cooler for a fee.
3. The company cleans and inspects the unit between renters; it does not touch the animal.

## Legal basis

- **Personal use:** 9 CFR 303.1(a)(1) exempts "slaughtering by any individual of livestock **of his own raising**, and the preparation **by him**" for his household ([9 CFR 303.1](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-reg-9cfr-303-1-custom-exemption.md)).
- **VDACS:** a buyer may use a farm's facilities to slaughter a purchased animal and it stays personal use; "**If the seller participates in the slaughter or processing activity, then the facility owner is subject to the custom exempt criteria and must have a Custom Permit.**" ([VDACS](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-reg-vdacs-red-meat-custom-exemptions.md)). That sentence addresses the seller who is also the facility owner; applying it to a third-party rental company is an analogy [inferred], so the renter's status should go to OMPS in writing. VDACS appears to read the buyer case more generously than the federal "own raising" text [inferred]: get its position in writing ([Minnesota comparison](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-participation-minnesota-31a15-exemption-wording.md)).
- **Comparison states** (persuasive only): Vermont defines "assist" as "the act of slaughtering or butchering" and excludes the selling farmer's provision of a site, implements, or offal disposal; by analogy [inferred], equipment rental alone is not assisting ([Vermont](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-participation-vermont-6vsa-3311a-personal-slaughter.md)); Maine authorizes renting a slaughterhouse, poultry only, and the renter must do the work ([Maine](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-participation-maine-22mrsa-2517d-slaughterhouse-rental.md)). In every text found, the test is **who performs the work**; payment is not an element [inferred].
- **Storage:** a fee-based locker for others is likely the "public warehouse" class: free 3-year VDACS Handlers Permit, records, OMPS visits; permit holders "may not open packages ... or further process" ([Handlers Permit](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-storage-vdacs-meat-poultry-handlers-permit.md); [§ 3.2-5404](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-storage-va-code-3-2-5404-warehouseman-registration.md)). Federal 9 CFR 320.5 registration applies only "in or for commerce" (interstate) ([9 CFR 320](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-storage-9cfr-320-records-registration.md)). Status for owner-held uninspected carcasses: **unverified, ask OMPS**.

## Zoning and site (Frederick County)

- Farm side: the RA slaughterhouse definition "shall not be construed to prohibit persons who are actually farmers from killing their own cattle ... for their own family use" ([RA](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-coolreg-frederick-county-zoning-ra.md)). A renter killing their own animal fits this exception cleanly; a renter who bought the animal is less clear [inferred].
- Yard and cooler: **B3** combines "Warehousing and Storage", "Car, Truck, Utility Trailers, and RV ... Rental and Leasing", truck repair and parking, all by right. A 53 ft semi is not a utility trailer, so the rental classification is uncertain; unit washing may be "Machinery Cleaning" (M1 only) ([Article VI](../../raw/data/2026-10-03-r4-zoning-frederick-county-article-vi-use-lists.md)).

## Waste

- Offal on the farm of generation fits 9VAC20-81-95 D.4 cleanly, because the renter is the farm ([DEQ](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-disposal-deq-vswmr-exemptions-compost.md)).
- Blood and wash water: the renter's problem on paper, but the rental agreement should require blood capture and a holding tank (the NC agreement makes the renter "solely responsible" for offal disposal) ([Orange/Durham agreement](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-rental-orange-durham-mpu-user-agreement.md)).
- Sanitation between renters falls on the company.

## Capacity and labor

- **No cutter-labor ceiling**: the renter does the work. The limit is rental days and units.
- Company labor: scheduling, delivery (if owner-towed), cleaning, inspection, storage handling.
- A 53 ft semi is not renter-towable (Class A CDL). Towable custom-exempt trailers ($72k-$115k) have no cutting room, so renters would still cut elsewhere [inferred].
- Precedent: **no red-meat unit in NMPAN's directory is rented DIY**; every red-meat MSU is owner-operated or run by a contracted processor. All rental programs found are poultry ([NMPAN directory](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-rental-nmpan-mobile-units-in-operation.md)).

## Economics

**Rough need [placeholder]:** about $100-120k/yr to cover about $56k debt service plus about $40-60k operations.

## What it fixes

- Removes the cutting-labor ceiling.
- Fits the RA farmer-family-use exception and D.4 offal rule cleanly.
- Cooler is storage only (B3 by right).

## What it costs

- **Market mismatch:** freezer-beef producers selling halves and quarters to other households cannot use personal-use; they would need their own (free) Custom Permit and its obligations.
- **Participation trap [inferred]:** the VDACS text covers a seller/facility owner who participates; by analogy, if staff help, the company is likely "participating" and a custom operator again. A third-party renter's status is for OMPS to settle (Option E questions 1-3).
- **Liability:** amateurs with a stunning firearm, winches, saws and grinders. Virginia voids pre-injury personal-injury waivers (*Hiett v. Lake Barcroft*, 244 Va. 191, 1992) ([Hiett](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-rentins-hiett-va-preinjury-waiver-void.md)). The agritourism inherent-risk statute (Va. Code § 3.2-6400 to 6402) never covers negligence and probably does not reach an off-site trailer rental ([agritourism](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-rentins-va-agritourism-liability-act.md)).
- **Insurance pattern:** rental programs insure only the unit and its transport; renters carry their own farm and product liability. Kentucky's $1M per-user requirement was "prohibitively expensive" and was dropped only after a university took ownership ([NMPAN insurance](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-rentins-nmpan-mppu-rental-insurance-models.md); [KSU](../../raw/articles/2026-10-03-r4-rental-kentucky-state-mppu-case-study.md)).
- **Demand for DIY beef processing is unverified.**

## Open questions

1. VDACS: does a renter who bought (not raised) the animal stay personal use? What counts as the company "participating"?
2. OMPS: does a cooler holding owner-held uninspected carcasses need a Handlers Permit?
3. Zoning Administrator: is renting a 53 ft processing semi "trailer rental" in B3?
4. Broker quote: rental-equipment GL plus inland marine on the unit.
5. Demand: how many Valley owners would kill and cut their own beef?

## Verdict

**Candidate, high uncertainty.** Best as a variant, not a standalone business: (1) an off-season rental add-on (Feb-Apr), including hogs, sheep and goats; or (2) two tiers, "you do it" rental plus storage and "we do it" full service under the operator's free Custom Permit. Demand and safety are the gaps that [[option-e-rental-plus-education|Option E]] ([Option E](option-e-rental-plus-education.md)) tries to close.
