# Option B: Cooler-Dock Kill-and-Cut

> **Status: chosen.** This is the model behind the
> [[mobile-beef-business-plan|Mobile Beef Processing Business Plan]] ([Mobile Beef Processing Business Plan](../topics/mobile-beef-business-plan.md)),
> the [business plan PDF](../../output/business-plan-mobile-beef-2026-10-02.pdf) and the site calculator.
> Full workup: [[cooler-backed-mobile-model|Cooler-Backed Mobile Model]] ([Cooler-Backed Mobile Model](../topics/cooler-backed-mobile-model.md)).
> All options: [[business-model-options|Business Model Options]] ([Business Model Options](../topics/business-model-options.md)). Not legal advice.

## How it works

1. The mobile unit kills on the owner's farm; offal stays there or goes to the renderer; the carcass chills on board.
2. Sides or quarters hang 7-21 days in the **company cooler**.
3. The same unit **docks at the cooler** to cut, grind, vac-pack and label "Not for Sale".
4. The owner picks up at the cooler.

## Legal basis

- Free VDACS Custom Permit. VDACS expressly allows a custom-exempt "mobile slaughter **and processing** unit" ([VDACS](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-reg-vdacs-red-meat-custom-exemptions.md)).
- Customers are owners of the live animal, including quarter and half buyers recorded before slaughter. See [[custom-exempt-share-sales|Custom-Exempt Share Sales]] ([Custom-Exempt Share Sales](../concepts/custom-exempt-share-sales.md)).

## Zoning and site (Frederick County)

From the RA ordinance (round 3) and the Article VI district use lists (round 4) ([RA](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-coolreg-frederick-county-zoning-ra.md); [Article VI](../../raw/data/2026-10-03-r4-zoning-frederick-county-article-vi-use-lists.md)):

| District | Cooler + cutting dock | Note |
|---|---|---|
| RA | **Conditional use** BB "Slaughterhouses" ("slaughtering or processing of meats") | Public hearings; § 165-204.17: 100 ft setbacks, under roof, screening, 20,000 sq ft cap |
| B2 General Business | **Not allowed** | No food manufacturing, no warehousing |
| B3 Industrial Transition | Not allowed | "Warehousing and Storage" by right, but no food manufacturing |
| TM, M1 | Not allowed | Food manufacturing "excluding ... Animal Slaughtering and Processing" |
| **M2 Industrial General** | **By right** | "Food Manufacturing including Animal Slaughtering and Processing" |

- Uses are classified by NAICS; cutting carcasses is NAICS 311612 under 3116 "Animal Slaughtering and Processing" [inferred mapping; the Zoning Administrator decides].
- M2 trade-offs [inferred]: likely public sewer (solves dock wash water), but no farm-building exemption (full commercial building permit, Va. Code § 36-99 applies only on farms) and higher land or lease cost.
- § 165-204.17 may apply to slaughterhouses in any district, M2 included: **unverified**.
- Detail: [[cooler-site-permits|Cooler Site Permits]] ([Cooler Site Permits](../concepts/cooler-site-permits.md)).

## Waste

- Farm side: offal composted on the farm of kill (likely exempt, 9VAC20-81-95 D.4) ([DEQ](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-disposal-deq-vswmr-exemptions-compost.md)); blood captured, not spread (DEQ "industrial wastes") ([VPA](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-water-9vac25-32-vpa-scope-exclusions.md)).
- Dock side: cutting and wash-down water at one fixed site; public sewer is the best route (Frederick Water 2025: $50.18/month base + $7.03-11.06 per 1,000 gal) ([Frederick Water](../../raw/data/2026-10-02-r3-water-frederick-water-sewer-specs-fees.md)).
- Detail: [[slaughter-waste-disposal-virginia|Slaughter Waste Disposal in Virginia]] ([Slaughter Waste Disposal in Virginia](../concepts/slaughter-waste-disposal-virginia.md)).

## Capacity and labor

- About **8 man-hours per custom beef** to cut (4.5 standardized) (NMPAN/Lorentz 2010) ([NMPAN costing](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-cutshop-nmpan-costing-throughput-cutter-productivity.md)).
- Two people: about **6-10 head/week**, about 300-400 head/yr [inferred].
- Virginia butcher wage: $20.03/hr mean (BLS May 2025) ([BLS](../../raw/data/2026-10-02-r3-cutshop-bls-oews-butcher-wages-va.md)).

## Economics

From the illustrative pro forma in the [business plan](../../output/business-plan-mobile-beef-2026-10-02.md) (sourced benchmarks plus labelled placeholders):

| Line | Value |
|---|---|
| Capex | **$471,576** (Sentinel 53 ft from $300k, 2026 list) |
| Debt service | $56,130/yr (80% financed at 8.5%, 10 yr; lender terms are a placeholder) |
| Revenue per head | about $800-1,100 (kill $100-150 + mileage; cut and pack $0.90-1.05/lb; 2025 benchmarks) [inferred] |
| Break-even | about 210 head/yr to cover debt service; about **274 head/yr** with a $50k owner draw |
| Cash before owner pay, Y1-Y3 | $17.8k / $70.7k / $129.5k at 200 / 300 / 375 head |

## What it fixes

- Removes the partner cut-shop dependency that idled other mobile units (see [[option-a-kill-only-partner-cut-shop|Option A]] ([Option A](option-a-kill-only-partner-cut-shop.md))).
- Captures the cutting revenue, which is most of the processing bill.
- One fixed site concentrates the dock wastewater where sewer can take it.

## What it costs

- Cutting labor becomes the ceiling, inside the business.
- Highest capex of the options; a 53 ft semi needs a Class A CDL.
- The cooler site is a processing use: CUP in RA or an M2 parcel.

## Open questions

1. Zoning Administrator determination: cooler + docked cutting unit; § 165-204.17 in M2.
2. M2 parcels with sewer in reach, and their cost.
3. VDACS OMPS: does the Custom Permit cover the docked cut room and the cooler?
4. Real quotes for the unit and the cooler.

## Verdict

**Chosen.** Still the plan of record. Round 4 tightens the siting answer to "M2 by right, or a CUP in RA", not "a commercial parcel".
