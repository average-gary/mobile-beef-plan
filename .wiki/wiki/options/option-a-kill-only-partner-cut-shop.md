# Option A: Kill-Only Mobile Unit + Partner Cut Shop

> **Status: superseded** (rounds 1-2 recommendation; replaced by
> [[option-b-cooler-dock-kill-and-cut|Option B: Cooler-Dock Kill-and-Cut]] ([Option B: Cooler-Dock Kill-and-Cut](option-b-cooler-dock-kill-and-cut.md))
> in round 3). Kept so the reasoning stays visible. All options:
> [[business-model-options|Business Model Options]] ([Business Model Options](../topics/business-model-options.md)).
> Not legal advice.

## How it works

1. A custom-exempt kill-only trailer goes to the owner's farm: stun, bleed, skin, eviscerate in the field.
2. Offal stays on that farm (composted) or is tubbed for the renderer.
3. Sides or quarters ride in the trailer's cooler to a **partner cut shop** (custom-exempt or USDA plant), which hangs, cuts and packs.
4. The owner picks up at the partner shop.

## Legal basis

- Free VDACS Custom Permit for the operator, renewed each June 30. Product is the owner's, marked "Not for Sale" ([VDACS](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-reg-vdacs-red-meat-custom-exemptions.md)).
- The partner shop must itself be able to take custom-exempt carcasses (its own Custom Permit or custom segregation inside an inspected plant).
- See [[regulatory-models-compared|Regulatory Models Compared]] ([Regulatory Models Compared](../concepts/regulatory-models-compared.md)).

## Zoning and site (Frederick County)

- **No fixed processing site.** The rig only needs a yard to park and wash.
- Open for all options: whether a mobile unit killing on RA farms is itself a "slaughterhouse" use. The RA definition says "establishments primarily engaged in the slaughtering or processing of meats", and its explicit exception covers only farmers killing their own animals for family use ([RA zoning](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-coolreg-frederick-county-zoning-ra.md)). Needs a Zoning Administrator determination.

## Waste

- Offal: composting on the farm of kill is likely exempt under 9VAC20-81-95 D.4 ([DEQ](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-disposal-deq-vswmr-exemptions-compost.md)).
- Blood and kill-floor wash water are DEQ "industrial wastes" ([VPA](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-water-9vac25-32-vpa-scope-exclusions.md)): capture blood on the rig.
- No cutting waste: that lands at the partner shop.
- Detail: [[slaughter-waste-disposal-virginia|Slaughter Waste Disposal in Virginia]] ([Slaughter Waste Disposal in Virginia](../concepts/slaughter-waste-disposal-virginia.md)).

## Capacity and labor

- Kill labor: about 7 beef per person per 8-hour day (NMPAN/Lorentz 2010) ([NMPAN costing](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-cutshop-nmpan-costing-throughput-cutter-productivity.md)).
- The real limit is **partner cut-shop slots**. Regional plants ran 125-150% of capacity and every one reported a cutter shortage (PEC 2021) ([PEC](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-capacity-pec-fuller-processor-study-2021.md)).

## Economics

| Capital recovery | About $44/head at 300 head/yr on a $92k rig over 7 years, before interest [inferred] | [[custom-exempt-mobile-rig|Custom-Exempt Mobile Rig]] ([Custom-Exempt Mobile Rig](../concepts/custom-exempt-mobile-rig.md)) |

The cutting revenue ($0.90-1.05/lb, the bulk of a beef's processing bill) goes to the partner, not the operator.

## What it fixes

- Lowest capex of any option; no fixed site, no cooler, no cutting labor to hire.
- No processing-site zoning question.

## What it costs

- **It feeds the bottleneck instead of relieving it.** Owners still wait for cut-shop slots.
- Precedent: California's Central Coast mobile unit sat **idle about 2 years** with no reliable hanging and cutting partner ([Central Coast](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-cutshop-nmpan-central-coast-mhu.md)).
- Thin revenue per head; the business lives on a kill fee plus minimums.

## Open questions

1. Will any Valley cut shop commit cooler space and slots to a mobile kill partner? (Not tested.)
2. RA "slaughterhouse" determination for farm kills (shared with all options).

## Verdict

**Superseded.** Cheap, but it does not shorten anyone's wait while cutters and cooler space are the binding constraint. Its one live use is as a component: the kill trailer could pair with a separately sited cut room (noted in [[mobile-beef-business-plan|Mobile Beef Processing Business Plan]] ([Mobile Beef Processing Business Plan](../topics/mobile-beef-business-plan.md))).
