# Business Model Options

> **Framing.** The research has one fixed premise: a custom-exempt service in the Shenandoah Valley that
> takes beef from the owner's farm to the owner's freezer, because regional plants are full (PEC 2021: 125-150% of
> capacity, cutter shortage at all 7 plants) ([PEC](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-capacity-pec-fuller-processor-study-2021.md)).
> The options differ on **who does the work, where it happens, and what the company owns**. Each has its own page
> with an identical structure. The business plan, PDF and calculator model **Option B**. Not legal advice; every
> number carries its source and year on the option page; [inferred] marks derived claims.

## How to add an option

Each new idea is one iteration:

1. **Option page.** Create `wiki/options/option-<letter>-<slug>.md` with frontmatter (`title`, `category: option`, `status`, `sources`, `created`, `updated`, `tags`, `summary`, `confidence`) and the standard sections, in order: How it works; Legal basis; Zoning and site (Frederick County); Waste; Capacity and labor; Economics; What it fixes; What it costs; Open questions; Verdict; See Also.
2. **Matrix row data.** Add a column to the matrix above and a line to Decision status. Change a status only when a decision is made.
3. **Exploration log.** Add a dated, numbered entry to [[exploration-log|Exploration Log]] ([Exploration Log](../references/exploration-log.md)): the question as asked, the short answer, what changed, links.
4. **Indexes.** Update `wiki/options/_index.md`, `wiki/_index.md`, the master `_index.md` and `log.md`; add any new raw sources to their `raw/<type>/_index.md`. Add an `<li>` with the right `option-badge` to the Business model options list in `site/index.html` (hand-written, not generated), and update its badge whenever a status changes.
5. **Commit and push** the iteration as one commit, so the site rebuilds.
