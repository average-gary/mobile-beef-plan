# SBA: no grants to start or expand a business

Primary source: the official SBA "Grants" funding-programs page, fetched 2026-10-12.

## Extracted facts (verbatim)

- "SBA does not provide grants for starting and expanding a business."
- "SBA provides grants to nonprofits, Resource Partners, and educational organizations. These grants aim to support entrepreneurship through counseling and training programs."
- "SBA only communicates from email addresses ending in @sba.gov. If you are contacted by someone claiming to be from SBA, check their address. If they are not using an official SBA email address, you should suspect and report fraud."

## Notes

- The page shows no last-updated date.
- This page has no veteran-specific grant program. SBA's veteran help, such as Veterans Business Outreach Centers (VBOCs) and Boots to Business, is grant-funded *to the resource partner*. The veteran gets free training, not cash. [inferred from the page's "Resource Partners" language plus general knowledge; VBOC specifics were not fetched this round.]

## Fit for this business [inferred]

- This kills the "grants for veterans" startup myth at the source. Plan capital should treat SBA as a lender or guarantor (7(a), 504) and as free counseling (SBDC, VBOC). SBA is not a grantor.
- Any outreach offering "SBA veteran grants" for the MSU or cooler is a red flag (see the FTC government-grant-scam alert file).
