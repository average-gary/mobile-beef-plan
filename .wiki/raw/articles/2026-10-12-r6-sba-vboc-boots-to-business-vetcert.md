## VBOC
- "SBA has 31 organizations participating in this cooperative agreement and serving as VBOCs." They offer "workshops, training, counseling, and mentorship" and refer clients "to a community partner, lender, or SBA program."
- Business plans: VBOCs help develop a plan covering "legal form of the business, equipment requirements and cost, organizational structure, a strategic plan, market analysis, and a financial plan." Mentorship includes "on-site visits" and review of "monthly financial statements".
- Mid-Atlantic Region listing: "Old Dominion University Veterans Business Outreach Center. Coverage: Virginia, West Virginia. Address: 112 Bank St., Norfolk, VA, 23510. Website: https://www.odu.edu/iie/vboc. Telephone: 757-683-5505. Email: vboc@odu.edu."
- The University of Maryland VBOC (Riverdale, MD; 301-405-6071) covers DC, MD, and DE only, not Virginia.
- ODU page: services include "Military Transitioning Services," "Business Counseling and Mentoring," "Entrepreneurial Training & Development," "Business Plan Preparation," "Assistance with Access to Capital." It lists no satellite office in northern Virginia or the Valley, and staff are on-site in Norfolk on Fridays only, 8:30-11:30 a.m. Cost isn't stated; SBA resource-partner counseling is generally free.

## Boots to Business (B2B)
- "an entrepreneurial education and training program offered by SBA as part of the Department of Defense's Transition Assistance Program (TAP)... open to service members (including National Guard and Reserve) and military spouses."
- Core: "foundational two-day, in-person course." Follow-on: "B2B: Revenue Readiness," online via Mississippi State University.
- "available free of charge at participating installations." B2B Reboot runs off-installation "for veterans of all eras, National Guard and Reserve members, and military spouses" (VBOC page).
- Other SBA-funded veteran programs (Feb 2026 blog): Entrepreneurship Bootcamp for Veterans (EBV, St. Joseph's University), VEP (Oklahoma State), VETRN, V-WISE (women veterans, Syracuse IVMF), Veteran Institute for Procurement. "Most programs are offered at no cost; however, some require veterans and military spouses to fund their own travel and fees."

## VetCert (VOSB / SDVOSB)
- The NDAA 2021 moved certification from VA to SBA "as of January 1, 2023."
- 13 CFR 128.200: VOSB "Not less than 51 percent owned and controlled by one or more veterans who reside in the United States" and small under part 121; SDVOSB is the same with service-disabled veterans. There is a spouse/caregiver exception for permanently and totally disabled veterans.
- sba.gov: SDVOSBs "compete for federal sole-source and set-aside contracts across the federal government"; VOSBs "have additional opportunities... at the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) under the VA's Vets First program."
- Surplus property: VOSBs "can access federally owned personal property no longer in use through the General Services Administration's (GSA) Federal Surplus Personal Property Donation Program", through the State Agency for Surplus Property (SASP). The firm must be in-state, "in 'certified' status in SBA's VetCert program database," and use the property in the normal course of business. It must not resell it, and the SASP collects a service fee.

## Fit notes [inferred]
- VetCert has no direct value for a custom-exempt processor. Custom-exempt meat can't be sold, so it can't be sold to federal buyers, and federal food contracts need USDA-inspected product.
- Surplus property could be worth getting VetCert for: federal surplus trucks, reefer units, stainless tables, generators, and pressure washers via Virginia's SASP. Availability is unpredictable, but certification costs no fee.
- The ODU VBOC is a 3+ hour drive away; expect virtual counseling. For in-person help near Winchester, the Shenandoah Valley SBDC (Lord Fairfax/Laurel Ridge area) is closer (not researched here). A VBOC-reviewed plan helps with both SBA lenders and the VR&E feasibility review (38 CFR 21.257(f) requires SBA coordination).
