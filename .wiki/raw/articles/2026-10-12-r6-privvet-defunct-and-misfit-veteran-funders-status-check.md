# Status check: commonly listed veteran funders, as of 2026-10-12

Each item below comes from fetching the program's own domain on 2026-10-12.

## Second Service Foundation: SHUT DOWN

- https://www.secondservicefoundation.org/ : "Following ten years of extraordinary impact on the military and veterans entrepreneurship community, the Second Service Foundation has been voluntarily shut down in order to transition into a new, faith-based mission."

## StreetShares Foundation / Veteran Small Business Award: DOMAIN HIJACKED

- https://streetsharesfoundation.org/ returns the page title "AGENHOKI88: Situs Judi Slot88 Resmi Online Slot Gacor Terbaik" (Indonesian online-slot gambling spam).
- The original foundation and award program are gone. **Do not submit personal data through any "StreetShares veteran grant" link.** Any listicle still sending readers there is stale, and the link now leads to a spam site.

## FedEx Small Business Grant: RETIRED

- https://www.fedex.com/en-us/small-business/grants.html (redirected from `/grant-contest.html`): "While the program is retired, you can still learn from their journeys." It ran 2012-2024.

## Comcast RISE: REBRANDED / REDIRECTED

- https://www.comcastrise.com/ redirects to https://corporate.comcast.com/company/impact/project-up. The page body would not render (cookie wall), so we could not confirm whether any small-business grant round is open [unverified].

## Bunker Labs: ACQUIRED BY SYRACUSE IVMF

- https://bunkerlabs.org/ redirects with a 301 to https://ivmf.syracuse.edu/. IVMF says: "The same great programs that Bunker Labs is known for will continue to be available for the military-community, but now within the IVMF Entrepreneurship portfolio." The page does not mention Veterans in Residence or CEOcircle by name.
- IVMF start-up programs: EBV ("Cutting edge bootcamp training program teaching the steps and stages of business creation"), EBV-F, EBV-WWP (with Wounded Warrior Project), V-WISE (women), and Military Founders Lab ("10-week virtual program"). IVMF says these are "at no cost for transitioning service members, veterans and military spouses."
- No cash grants are advertised. This is training and network only.

## Hivers & Strivers: REAL, BUT EQUITY ANGEL FUND

- https://hiversandstrivers.com/ : "To provide early-stage financing to ventures led by U.S. military veterans", "seed stage through Series B", "industry agnostic".
- About page: check sizes "$100K-1M", "$80M" AUM, "8" exits, "60+% IRR".
- The portfolio page lists companies such as BoodleBox, Oply, Vulcan Elements, ReturnLogic, LeaseLock, Independence Hydrogen, Polco, OutFit, and True Made Foods (a food brand). There are no farms, processors, or service businesses.

## Bob Woodruff Foundation: NOT A FUNDER OF INDIVIDUALS (unverified on this fetch)

- bobwoodrufffoundation.org blocked fetches with a Cloudflare 403. From prior general knowledge (not verified here), BWF makes grants to nonprofit organizations serving veterans, not to individuals or for-profit businesses [unverified].

## Hello Alice: NOT FETCHED

- helloalice.com returned 403/429 (Vercel bot checkpoint). Hello Alice does run real sponsor-funded small-business grant rounds, sometimes for military and veteran owners, but current 2026 rounds, amounts, and dates are [unverified]. Check the site manually.

## Fit notes [inferred]

- Remove all of the above from any funding plan as cash sources. IVMF EBV training is worth taking because it is free, and it complements Warrior Rising.
- Hivers & Strivers would only matter if the business became a scalable multi-unit company. A single custom-exempt mobile unit is not a venture-scale deal.
