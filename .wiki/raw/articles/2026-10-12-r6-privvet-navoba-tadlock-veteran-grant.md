# Tadlock Veteran Business Grant (NaVOBA partner grant)

Pages fetched 2026-10-12:
- https://www.navoba.org/stephen-l-tadlock-veteran-grant (NaVOBA page, still shows the 2025 timeline)
- https://foundersfirstcdc.org/stephen-tadlock (administrator page, shows the 2026 timeline)

## 2025 cycle (NaVOBA page)

- "Stephen L. Tadlock $25,000 grant for veteran-owned businesses". "NaVOBA is proud to partner with Founders First Capital".
- Timeline: grant live September 9; "The application deadline is October 7, 2025"; "The Finalist will be chosen on October 28, 2025" (one finalist).
- Eligibility: "Must be a U.S. military veteran"; "Must be a CEO, President or Business Owner"; "Must have an active U.S. based business"; "Business annual revenue does not exceed $5M"; "Must have between 2 to 100 employees/contracted"; "Minimum of 1 year in business".

## 2026 cycle (Founders First CDC page)

- "$25,000 Total Fund / 25 Finalists / $1,000+ Award + Program Access".
- "$1,000 Micro-Grant Flexible funding you can put toward the needs that matter most — equipment, operations, staffing, or growth."
- Finalists also get one free program: Passport Community ("$1,499 for a 3-month subscription (free for finalists)") or Zebra Leadership ("$1,699 (free for finalists)").
- Dates: "Jul 1, 2026 Pre-registration opens / Sept 15, 2026 Full application opens / Oct 15, 2026 Application closes / Oct 22, 2026 Semifinalists announced / Nov 10, 2026 Finalists announced".
- Eligibility: "Must be a U.S. military veteran or military spouse"; owner/CEO; active US business; revenue ≤ $5M; "between 2 to 100 employees"; "Minimum of 1 year in business".
- Payout: funds go out electronically through Bill.com after the recipient submits a W-9, signed terms, and program enrollment.

## NaVOBA itself

- A 501(c)(3) that certifies VBE/SDVBE businesses for corporate supplier diversity: "at least 51% owned, operated, and controlled by one or more U.S. military veterans." The homepage lists no other grants. Certification cost was not shown [unverified].

## Fit notes [inferred]

- The structure changed from one $25k winner (2025) to 25 winners of $1k each (2026). Treat any listicle that still advertises "$25,000 to a veteran business" for 2026 as out of date.
- Fails eligibility at launch: under 1 year in business, and likely fewer than 2 employees. Earliest realistic cycle is fall 2027 or later.
- VBE certification matters for corporate procurement, not for a custom-exempt service that sells to individual cattle owners. Low value for this business.
