# MPU Program User Agreement and Waiver (Durham/Orange, NC)

Footer: "Approved DCo 09.19.2022"; PDF filename dated 09.15.2023. Linked from the Orange County Center MPPU page (updated Jun 30, 2026), which says the agreement is "completed by staff and signed at MPPU pick up."

## Purpose clause
"The Mobile Processing Unit (MPU) is intended expressly for on-farm processing of poultry for home use and/or for sale under the North Carolina inspection exemption."

## Numbered terms (paraphrased closely; quotes where exact)
1. User has "sole responsibility to know and comply with all state and local laws and regulations governing food safety, including but not limited to those relating to state inspection exemptions."
2. User "solely responsible for proper operation of the MPU and for proper disposal of offal (guts, body, head, feathers, etc.)."
3. User solely responsible for operation and "agrees to pay for any damages to the MPU which occur during transportation or operation."
4. User "assumes all risks of personal injury, death, and property damage or loss from any cause whatsoever, including ... his own conduct and the negligence of anyone else."
5. No possession until page 2 (waiver/checkout) is signed.
6. "The trailer hitch accepts only a 2" ball." (renter tows)
7. "Rental rates are $50.00 per 3-day reservation or $75.00 per 5-day reservation, payable at time of reservation." Extensions must be requested before the period ends; "There will be an additional charge for any approved extension." (amount not stated)
8. "A cleaning charge of $50 will be assessed if the unit is not properly cleaned and the user will be required to participate in additional training prior to the next rental. The user can be prohibited from future use of the MPU if there is more than one return in an unclean condition or the user does not pay the cleaning charge within 14 days of assessment."

## Checkout inventory (check-out / check-in quantities with staff initials)
8 large poultry cones; kill cone stand and base; Featherman scalder; Featherman plucker; Roto-Dunker; stainless table; eviscerating table; trash barrel; 2 kitchen trash cans; supply crate; transformer (110-220v); NSF water hose (75 ft).

## Release and indemnity (page 2)
User, "for myself, my heirs, executors, and assigns," releases and waives claims, and agrees to "indemnify, defend and save harmless Durham County, Orange County, NC State University, the State of North Carolina, and their respective trustees, officers, agents, and employees ... from all liabilities, losses, costs, damages, claims or causes of action ... including attorneys' fees," for injuries/damage to the user and to others arising from the user's operation. "I understand that I am fully responsible for my own safety, my own insurance coverage, and any other risk management measures applicable to my use of the MPU."

Recorded fields: name, signature, address, phone, rental period, actual pickup and drop-off date/time, representative signature.

## Observations [inferred]
- Insurance: no certificate of insurance, named-insured endorsement, or proof of auto coverage is demanded; risk is shifted by waiver/indemnity alone. A private (non-government) rental business in Virginia would likely need its own commercial general liability and inland-marine/equipment coverage, since a waiver is weaker protection than sovereign/government status.
- Note the cleaning fee discrepancy: the agreement says $50; the 2026 Orange web page states only "a cleaning charge" with no amount; Rowan's page sets $100.
- The model is "renter does the work": the program provides equipment only, so the lender is not the slaughterer. For red meat in Virginia this distinction matters for custom-exempt status (owner-slaughter vs. custom slaughter for hire).
