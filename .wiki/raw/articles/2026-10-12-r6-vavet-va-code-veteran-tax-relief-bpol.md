# Virginia veteran tax relief vs. this business

All statutes fetched from law.lis.virginia.gov on 2026-10-12. This is primary law.

## § 58.1-3219.5: disabled veteran real property exemption
- "the General Assembly hereby exempts from taxation the real property ... of any veteran who has been rated by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs ... to have a 100 percent service-connected, permanent, and total disability, and who occupies the real property as his principal place of residence."
- Acreage: "the land, not exceeding one acre, upon which it is situated. However, if a county, city, or town provides for an exemption from or deferral of real property taxes of more than one acre of land pursuant to Article 2 (§ 58.1-3210 et seq.), then the county, city, or town shall also provide an exemption for the same number of acres." Frederick County's Article 2 acreage was not checked (unverified).
- **Outbuildings (key for the cooler):** "A real property improvement other than a dwelling ... shall also be exempt from taxation so long as the principal use of the improvement is (i) to house or cover motor vehicles or household goods and personal effects ... and (ii) for other than a business purpose."
- Leaseholds are excluded ("does not include any interest held under a leasehold or term of years"). Surviving spouses qualify if they do not remarry (subsection B).
- § 58.1-3219.6: the veteran files an affidavit with the local commissioner of the revenue.

## § 58.1-3668: disabled veteran motor vehicle
- "'motor vehicle' means only a passenger car or a pickup or panel truck ... that is registered for personal use."
- "one motor vehicle owned and used primarily by or for a veteran ... rated ... 100 percent service-connected, permanent, and total disability shall be exempt from taxation." Effective from 2021-01-01 or acquisition.

## § 58.1-3703: BPOL license taxes
- A WebFetch read of the full section found **no** provision referencing veterans, military, or disability. Subsection C (21 items) covers carriers, farmers selling their own products, nonprofits, and so on.
- C.2 exempts "selling farm or domestic products ... provided such products are grown or produced by the person offering them". This covers producers, not a custom slaughter service [inferred].
- Fee cap (A): license fee up to $100 (locality pop. >50,000), $50 (25,000-50,000), $30 (<25,000). Frederick County (~90k+) and Winchester (~28k) population tiers are [inferred] from general knowledge, not fetched.
- **(D) New business incentive (optional, local):** "Any county, city or town may establish by ordinance a business license incentive program for 'qualifying businesses' ... a business that locates for the first time in the locality ... may extend for a period not to exceed two years ... (i) an exemption, in whole or in part, of license taxes ... (ii) a refund or rebate ..."
- **(E) No-profit exemption (optional, local):** "any locality may exempt, by ordinance, license fees or license taxes on any business that does not have an after-tax profit for the taxable year and offers the income tax return of the business as proof to the local commissioner of the revenue."
- No section titled "veteran BPOL exemption" was found. The brief's assumption that § 58.1-3703 has a veteran exemption is **not supported** by the current text.

- Cooler siting: if the owner is 100% P&T and puts the cooler on the homestead parcel, the house and 1 acre stay exempt, but the cooler building would be assessed and taxed as a business improvement. Mixing the cooler into the exempt acre could also complicate the affidavit. A separate parcel or a lease is cleaner.
