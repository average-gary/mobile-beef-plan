# 9 CFR Part 320 — Records, Registration, and Reports

Authority: 21 U.S.C. 601-695. Source: 35 FR 15603, Oct. 3, 1970, as amended (latest amendments to 320.2/320.3 at 80 FR 79250, Dec. 21, 2015; 320.5 last amended 69 FR 254, Jan. 5, 2004). Implements FMIA 21 U.S.C. 642 (recordkeeping) and 643 (registration).

## § 320.1 Records required to be kept — who

"(a) Every person ... within any of the classes specified in paragraph (a) (1), (2), or (3) of this section is required by the Act to keep records which will fully and correctly disclose all transactions involved in his or its business subject to the Act:"

- "(1) Any person that engages, for commerce, in the business of slaughtering any cattle ... or preparing, freezing, packaging, or labeling any carcasses, or parts or products of carcasses ... for use as human food or animal food;"
- "(2) Any person that engages in the business of buying or selling (as a meat broker, wholesaler, or otherwise), or transporting in commerce, or **storing in or for commerce**, or importing, any carcasses, or parts or products of carcasses, of any such animals;"
- (3) renderers and dead/dying/disabled/diseased livestock dealers.

## § 320.1(b) Required records

"Records, such as bills of sale, invoices, bills of lading, and receiving and shipping papers, giving the following information with respect to each transaction in which any livestock or carcass, part thereof, meat or meat food product is purchased, sold, shipped, received, transported, or otherwise handled":
(i) name or description of the article; (ii) net weight; (iii) number of outside containers; (iv) buyer and seller name/address; (v) consignee/receiver; (vi) method of shipment; (vii) date of shipment; (viii) carrier name/address.

(b)(4) Raw ground beef: official establishments and retail stores keep supplier establishment numbers, lot numbers, production dates, date/time of each lot and of grinder cleaning — including (b)(4)(ii) product "ground at an individual customer's request."

## § 320.2 / 320.3 Location and retention

- Records kept "at the place where such business is conducted" (or headquarters if multiple locations), "in a safe place ... in accordance with good commercial practices."
- Retention: "2 years after December 31 of the year in which the transaction ... occurred" (plus longer if FSIS gives written notice for an investigation). Ground-beef records under (b)(4): one year.

## § 320.4 Access

Representatives of the Secretary "must be afforded any necessary facilities ... for the examination and copying of records and for the examination and sampling of inventory."

## § 320.5 Registration — operative text

"(a) Except as provided in paragraph (c) of this section, every person that engages in business in or for commerce, as a meat broker, renderer, or animal food manufacturer, or engages in business in commerce as a wholesaler of any carcasses, or parts or products of the carcasses, or any livestock ... or **engages in business as a public warehouseman storing any such articles in or for commerce**, or engages in the business of buying, selling, or transporting in commerce ... any dead, dying, disabled, or diseased livestock ... shall register with the Administrator, giving such information as is required, including his name, and the address of each place of business at which, and all trade names under which he conducts such business, by filing with the Administrator, Food Safety and Inspection Service ... a form containing such information within 90 days ... after such later date as he begins to engage in such business."

"(b) Whenever any change is made in the name of, or address of any place of business ... or any trade name ... he shall report such change in writing to the Administrator within 15 days after making the change."

"(c) The registration requirements prescribed in this section shall not apply to persons conducting any of the businesses specified in this section only at an official establishment."

[The registration form is FSIS Form 5020-1, "Registration of Meat and Poultry Handlers" — inferred from FSIS practice; not named in the regulation text.]

## Relevance to a fee-based cooler/locker for other people's custom-exempt meat [inferred]

- Federal registration/recordkeeping under 320.1(a)(2) and 320.5 is keyed to "in or for commerce" (interstate/foreign commerce as defined in FMIA 21 U.S.C. 601(h)). A Virginia locker storing Virginia farmers' custom-exempt beef for Virginia owners is generally intrastate, and Virginia's mirror statute (Va. Code 3.2-5400 et seq.) governs instead.
- Neither section prohibits storing custom-exempt ("Not for Sale") product; they impose registration and recordkeeping on the storer. The custom-exempt product's restrictions (owner's household use only, no sale, "Not for Sale" marking, segregation) come from 9 CFR 303.1(a)(2) and (b), already in the wiki.
- If the cooler is run inside an official (inspected) establishment, the 320.5(c) exemption from separate registration applies.

## Statutory basis (FMIA, via law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/21/642, /643, /601)

- 21 U.S.C. 642(a) (FMIA §202, Pub. L. 90-201, 1967): the listed classes "shall keep such records as will fully and correctly disclose all transactions involved in their businesses; and ... shall, at all reasonable times upon notice by a duly authorized representative of the Secretary, afford such representative access to their places of business and opportunity to examine the facilities, inventory, and records thereof, to copy all such records, and to take reasonable samples of their inventory upon payment of the fair market value therefor." Class (2): "Any persons, firms, or corporations that engage in the business of ... storing in or for commerce ... any carcasses, or parts or products of carcasses."
- 21 U.S.C. 643 (FMIA §203): "No person, firm, or corporation shall ... engage in business as a public warehouseman storing any such articles in or for commerce ... unless, when required by regulations of the Secretary, he has registered with the Secretary his name, and the address of each place of business at which, and all trade names under which, he conducts such business."
- 21 U.S.C. 601(h): "The term 'commerce' means commerce between any State, any Territory, or the District of Columbia, and any place outside thereof" — i.e., interstate. Intrastate storage falls outside these federal sections.
