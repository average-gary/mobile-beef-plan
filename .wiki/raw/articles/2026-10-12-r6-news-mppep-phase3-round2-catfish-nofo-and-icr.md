# MPPEP news, 2026: catfish-only round plus an ICR extension

Sources: the grants.gov opportunity 363804 (fetched through the grants.gov REST API, `fetchOpportunity`), the attached "MPPEP NOFO Final.pdf", and Federal Register doc 2026-10137 (https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/21/2026-10137/...). The rd.usda.gov page for this round returned HTTP 403 and was not fetched: https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/business-programs/meat-and-poultry-processing-expansion-program-phase-3

## Grants.gov search result (2026-10-12)

- grants.gov keyword searches for "slaughter", "meat", "processing capacity", "livestock processing", and "veteran farmer" (posted or forecasted) returned **only one USDA meat-processing opportunity**: RD-RBS-26-03-MPPEP, "MPPEP Phase 3 Invasive Wild-Caught Catfish," open 09/08/2026, close 12/07/2026, CFDA 10.381.
- No FY2027 general meat and poultry processing NOFO (beef, red meat, MSU) was posted or forecast as of the fetch date. An adjacent opening: the RBCS Agriculture Innovation Center grant (RBCS-2026-AIC), open 09/09/2026 to 01/31/2027. It is not a processor grant and its eligibility was not checked.

## NOFO facts

- Funding: "$6 million" aggregate; awardCeiling $2,000,000; awardFloor $50,000; about 6 awards.
- "A cost share of at least 20 percent of the total project cost is required." If the cost share is not verified in the letter-of-conditions window, "the Agency may offer funding to the next highest ranked project."
- "Costs and activities that support existing Processing capacity are not eligible." Expenses must fall between execution of Form RD 4280-2 and the end of the period of performance, which can be up to 48 months. No pre-award costs.
- Eligibility includes for-profit entities and small businesses. Each applicant "must comply with FSIS standards and must possess or plan to obtain a Federal Grant of Inspection during the period of performance."
- Applicants must disclose five years of health, safety, and labor violations and any inspection suspensions.
- Disbursement options:
  1. "Single Disbursement - Grant funds will be disbursed in full after the project is 100 percent completed and a valid grant of inspection (Federal or equivalent) is provided."
  2. "Reimbursement - ... through 90 percent of the grant disbursement. The final 10 percent of the grant funds will be held until construction or installation of the project is completed and a valid grant of inspection..." Requests can be made at most monthly, are paid "Ordinarily... within 30 days," and need evidence of payment: "Failure to provide sufficient documentation of costs and evidence of payment may result in a denial of reimbursement."
  3. Advance payments are limited to "minimum amounts needed." Unspent advances "must be returned." Interest above $500 per year must be returned.
- Equipment disposition: "Awardees must request disposition instruction from the Agency if equipment purchased with grant funds is no longer needed for its original purpose" (2 CFR 200.313). This is a federal-interest string on grant-bought equipment.
- Scoring: Alignment 20, Market 20, Financial Viability 20, Technical Merit 20 (includes "access to operating capital risk" and ability to "maintain expanded operations after the period of performance ends"), Labor 10, Community Support 10, Discretionary up to 15 ("diversity among awards on the basis of geography and species, including the prioritization of projects involving the Processing of Apex Predators").
- **Veteran priority: none.** A text search of the NOFO for "veteran" found the word only in the appropriations act title. The NOFO also has no socially-disadvantaged or beginning-farmer criteria.

## Federal Register PRA notice (91 FR 29933-29934, May 21, 2026)

- RBCS "intention to request an extension of a currently approved information collection package for the Meat and Poultry Processing Expansion Program (MPPEP)." Docket RBS-26-Business-0199. Comments were due July 20, 2026.
- OMB 0570-0079. Estimated 233 respondents, 12 responses each, 2,796 responses, 12,049 burden hours, 4.31 hours per response.
- Respondents listed include "for-profit entities."
- Interpretation [inferred]: an ICR extension keeps the application forms legally usable. It does **not** appropriate money or announce a round.

## Fit for this business [inferred]

- Not usable: the round is catfish-only and requires a grant of inspection.
- Pattern: three MPPEP features would still block or burden a custom-exempt beef MSU in any future beef round. The inspection requirement excludes custom-exempt. The 10% holdback and single-disbursement options pay only at completion. The 20% cash match comes on top.
- Veteran status earns nothing in MPPEP scoring as currently written.
