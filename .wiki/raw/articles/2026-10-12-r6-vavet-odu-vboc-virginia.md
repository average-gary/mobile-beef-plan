# ODU VBOC (Virginia/West Virginia)

## Coverage (SBA resource partner list, https://www.sba.gov/counseling/local-assistance/resource-partners/#veterans-business-outreach-centers, fetched 2026-10-12)
- "Mid-Atlantic Region: Old Dominion University Veterans Business Outreach Center. Coverage: Virginia, West Virginia. Address: 112 Bank St., Norfolk, VA, 23510. Website: https://www.vbocvirginia.com/ Telephone: 757-683-5505 Email: vboc@odu.edu"
- SBA: VBOCs "conduct Boots to Business classes for service members and military spouses" and "B2B Reboot classes off installations for veterans of all eras, National Guard and Reserve members, and military spouses."

## vbocvirginia.com (fetched 2026-10-12, (c)2026)
- "Free, veteran-focused guidance to start your business."
- Boots to Business: "Two-day, no cost, interactive workshop; One-on-one follow-up with veteran advisors; Action plan tailored specifically to your goals; Access to Small Business Administration resources."
- "Every service is 100 % free — no hidden fees." The FAQ adds that some special trainings or seminars may carry a cost.
- Menu: 1-on-1 coaching, advising, mentor directory, Reboot, Small Business Certifications, "Boots to Business Revenue Readiness" (page content empty when fetched).
- Eligible clients (FAQ, partially garbled in extraction): active-duty service members, veterans, service-disabled veterans, Guard/Reserve, and dependents, including TAP-eligible transitioning service members.
- "Funded in part through a Cooperative Agreement with the U.S. Small Business Administration."

## Funding Guides page (https://www.vbocvirginia.com/funding-guides)
- Outdated: it centers on the 2020 CARES Act (PPP, EIDL, SBA Express Bridge). It also lists the Virginia Department of Business Assistance, an agency since merged (now SBSD), and Hampton Roads economic development offices. **No veteran grant or loan program** is listed. Do not rely on it for current funding.

## Fit notes [inferred]
- No capital, only coaching. Good for a veteran owner who wants veteran-peer mentors and help with SBA 7(a)/Express or SBA Veterans Advantage lender introductions.
- The VBOC can also coach federal VA VetCert SDVOSB/VOSB certification. That is only relevant for federal contracting, which a custom-exempt processor does not do.
