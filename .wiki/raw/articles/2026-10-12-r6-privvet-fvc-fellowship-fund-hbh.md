# Farmer Veteran Coalition (FVC): Fellowship Fund and Homegrown By Heroes

Primary pages fetched 2026-10-12:
- https://farmvetco.org/fvfellowship/
- https://farmvetco.org/fvfellowship/2026-farmer-veteran-fellowship-fund-awardees/
- https://farmvetco.org/hbh/
- https://farmvetco.org/programs/funding-opportunities/

Note: the older URL `farmvetco.org/fellowshipfund` now returns 404.

## Fellowship Fund: terms

- Award size: "$1,000 to $5,000".
- Track record: "nearly $4 million has been awarded to more than 930 veterans since 2011".
- **No cash to the veteran.** "the Fellowship Fund manager makes the payment on the farmer veteran's behalf" to third-party vendors for items the veteran identifies.
- Example purchases: "All-Terrain Vehicles (ATV)," "Beekeeping equipment," "Breeding livestock," "Bulk feed," "Fencing," "Scholarships," "Storage freezers," "Tractor implements," "Walk-behind tractors."
- The funding-opportunities page describes it as "a small grant for the purchase of critical farm equipment or infrastructure."

## Eligibility (quoted)

- Must be a "member of Farmer Veteran Coalition".
- "Have served or are currently serving in any branch of the United States Military".
- Received an "'Honorable' characterization of service upon discharge".
- "Must have an agricultural business in operation and a business plan".
- "The Fellowship is designed to support for profit agricultural businesses".
- Past recipients may reapply only "if previously awarded $1,000 or less".

## Cycle

- Opens "once per year". Applicants get "no less than four (4) weeks". The funding page says "typically in the late fall or early winter".
- Application: short answer and essay questions, plus an uploaded "business plan" and "Proof of service".
- Review takes "up to two months". Awardees are "usually being notified in the Spring".
- Recipients have "six (6) months to use their award".
- Exact 2026-27 open dates are not posted on the pages fetched [unverified; check the page in roughly Nov 2026].

## 2026 awardees

- About 120 awardees across sponsor categories. Some are tied to sponsors, such as an FVC Virginia Chapter tranche and Tractor Supply gift certificates.
- Virginia: Bryan Harris (Liberty Lane Farm, Suffolk) and William Marm (Legacy Peony Farm LLC, Manassas) were funded under the FVC Virginia Chapter. Christopher Mulder (Mulder Family Farm, Alexandria) received a Tractor Supply gift certificate.
- Several cattle operations appear on the list, for example VVV Ranch & Cattle Company LLC and BFE Cattle Co. **No meat-processing or butchery business appears on the list.**

## Homegrown By Heroes (HBH) label

- Veteran eligibility: any branch or era, including current service members; "Honorable" or "General (Under Honorable Conditions)" discharge.
- Business: "a minimum of 50% veteran ownership and a minimum of 50% veteran management control."
- Value-added products qualify if they "contain a minimum of 50% non-water ingredients produced by an HBH-certified producer".
- Renewal: "every three years". The fetched page does not mention any fee.
- The page has no specific rules for meat.

## Fit notes [inferred]

- The fund is aimed at farms. Whether a custom-exempt kill/cut service counts as an "agricultural business" is unconfirmed. Ask FVC before applying.
- The program requires a business already "in operation", so a pre-launch startup probably does not qualify until it is running.
- Customers own the carcasses under custom exemption, and the service does not sell product, so the HBH label is of limited use to the processor. It is more relevant to veteran producer clients who sell their own beef.
- The FVC Virginia Chapter is a possible networking and referral channel to veteran cattle producers, who are potential customers.
