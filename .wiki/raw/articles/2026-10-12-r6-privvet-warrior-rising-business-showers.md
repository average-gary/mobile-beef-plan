# Warrior Rising: Warrior University and Business Showers

Pages fetched 2026-10-12: https://warriorrising.org/, https://warriorrising.org/warrior-university/, https://warriorrising.org/business-showers/

## Organization

- Describes itself as "a 501(c)(3) Non-Profit" for veteran entrepreneurs. Contact: info@warriorrising.org, 1-833-932-7397.
- Programs: LaunchPoint (SDVET) ("learn how to take an idea and turn it into a viable business opportunity"); Warrior Academy ("Take your idea and turn it into a business plan on your own time"); Warrior University ("Advanced training for established and growing businesses"); Business Showers ("Live pitch competitions to secure funding with no strings attached").

## Warrior University

- "Veterans, military spouses, and immediate family members with an early-stage startup who are ready for advanced training".
- "offered over two evenings", "offered quarterly and fully virtual".
- Covers customer discovery, competitive analysis, and go-to-market planning. "Graduates earn a certificate of completion and get the opportunity to compete for grant funding at our signature Business Showers."
- Cost is not stated on the page [unverified].

## Business Showers

- "Top finalists walk away with non-dilutive grants from $2,000 to $150,000. No equity. No catch."
- "Graduates of Warrior University may apply for Business Showers. Only the most competitive are selected".
- "invitation-only, in-person pitch competitions held throughout the country, in cities such as Cedar Rapids, Washington D.C., and Salt Lake City".
- Includes "Flights and hotel covered; individualized pitch coaching; live pitch competition; non-dilutive grants $2K–$150K; investor & decision-maker access".
- Not stated: number of winners per event, typical award, 2026 schedule, whether funds are paid as cash or to vendors, and any membership fee [all unverified].

## Fit notes [inferred]

- Eligibility covers immediate family members, so the owner may be able to qualify through a family member even without confirmed veteran status. Confirm with Warrior Rising.
- Path: Warrior Academy, then Warrior University (next quarterly cohort), then apply to a Showers event. Allow several months.
- The $150K figure is a ceiling. Do not budget it. Plan around the low end ($2K-$25K) at most, and only as upside.
- Older web reports (not verified here) said Warrior Rising charged membership or program fees. Check current terms before enrolling.
