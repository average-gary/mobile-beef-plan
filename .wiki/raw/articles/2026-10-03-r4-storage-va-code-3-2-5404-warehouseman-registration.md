# Va. Code Title 3.2, Chapter 54 — storage, records, and warehouseman registration provisions

Chapter 54 as a whole is already in the wiki; this file extracts the subsections specific to a business that stores other people's meat for a fee.

## § 3.2-5404 Duties of the Board (operative text)

**Subdivision 7 — recordkeeping and access.** The Board "Shall, by regulations, require that the following classes of persons shall keep such records and for such periods as are specified in the regulations to fully and correctly disclose all transactions involved in their business, and afford to the Commissioner access to such places of business, an opportunity, at all reasonable times, to examine the facilities, inventory and records thereof, to copy the records, and to take reasonable samples of the inventory upon payment of the fair market value therefor: any persons that engage in or for intrastate commerce: (i) in the business of slaughtering any livestock or poultry, or preparing, freezing, packaging or labeling, buying or selling (as brokers, wholesalers or otherwise), transporting, or **storing any livestock products** or poultry products for human or animal food; or (ii) in business as renderers ..."

**Subdivision 9 — storage and handling conditions.** The Board "Shall, by regulations, prescribe conditions of storage and handling of livestock products and poultry products by persons engaged in the business of buying, selling, freezing, storing, or transporting such articles in or for intrastate commerce to assure that such articles will not be adulterated or misbranded when delivered to the consumer."

**Subdivision 10 — registration.** The Board "Shall, by regulations, require that every person engaged in business in or for intrastate commerce as a broker, renderer, animal food manufacturer, or wholesaler or **public warehouseman of livestock products** or poultry products, or engaged in the business of buying, selling or transporting in intrastate commerce, any dead, dying, disabled or diseased livestock or poultry ... shall register with the Commissioner his name and the address of each place of business at which and all trade names under which he conducts such business."

**Subdivision 11** — the Board "May adopt by reference or otherwise such provisions of the rules and regulations under the federal acts ... that shall have the same force and effect as if promulgated under this article."

**Subdivision 3** (context) — the Board "Shall prohibit the entry into official establishments of livestock products and poultry products not prepared under federal inspection or inspection pursuant to this article." [inferred: a cooler/locker co-located inside a Virginia-inspected official establishment cannot take in uninspected custom-exempt carcasses except as the 2VAC5-210 / 9 CFR 303.1 custom-exempt segregation rules allow.]

History: 1970, c. 290, § 3.1-884.20; 1991, c. 344; 2008, c. 860.

## § 3.2-5405(A)(9) Exemption power

The Commissioner may "Exempt the operations of any person from inspection or other requirements of this article if and to the extent such operations would be exempt from the corresponding requirements under the federal acts if they were conducted in or for interstate commerce."

## § 3.2-5406 Adoption by reference

The Commissioner may adopt "by reference any regulation under the federal acts as it pertains to this chapter, amending it as necessary for intrastate applicability." (1991 c. 344; 2008 c. 860.) [inferred: this is the route by which 9 CFR 320 recordkeeping/registration applies intrastate via 2VAC5-210.]

## § 3.2-5418 Federal preemption limit

"The requirements of this chapter shall apply to persons, establishments, animals, and articles regulated under the Federal Meat Inspection Act ... only to the extent provided for in said federal acts."

## Relevance to a fee-based cooler/locker [inferred]

- A Frederick County cooler that holds hanging custom-exempt carcasses or frozen cuts for local owners for a fee is "storing livestock products" in intrastate commerce: expect (a) registration with the VDACS Commissioner as a public warehouseman, (b) transaction records with VDACS access, (c) compliance with whatever storage/handling conditions the Board sets.
- Nothing here prohibits storing custom-exempt product that belongs to the animal's owner; the product's "Not for Sale"/owner-use-only status from 9 CFR 303.1 travels with it. Whether VDACS treats a locker holding only owner-held custom product as a "public warehouseman" vs. an extension of the custom-exempt operation is not answered by the statute — confirm with VDACS Office of Meat and Poultry Services.
