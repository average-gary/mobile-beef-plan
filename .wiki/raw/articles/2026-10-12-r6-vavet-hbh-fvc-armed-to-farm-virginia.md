# Veteran ag-branding and training programs relevant to Virginia

## Homegrown By Heroes (https://farmvetco.org/hbh/, fetched 2026-10-12)
- "The Homegrown By Heroes label (HBH) is the official farmer veteran branding program of America and is administered nationally by the Farmer Veteran Coalition. It certifies ranchers, farmers, and fisherman of all military eras to sell their product as veteran owned and produced."
- Eligibility: "Veterans of any branch of the U.S. Military from all service eras–including those currently serving"; discharge "'Honorable' or 'General (Under Honorable Conditions)'"; "Agribusinesses and farming operations that maintain a minimum of 50% veteran ownership and a minimum of 50% veteran management control"; value-added products must have "a minimum of 50% non-water ingredients produced by an HBH-certified producer."
- "you must first be a member of the Farmer Veteran Coalition." The page cites "more than 2,000" certified producers (another paragraph says 1,800+ members in all 50 states). An older 2015 FVC post says the program "is free of charge".
- State partners: "Several State Departments of Agriculture have officially partnered with the Homegrown By Heroes label to produce a merged label combining elements of the state's own 'Buy Local' label ... you must first become certified with the Homegrown By Heroes label before certifying with your state's department of agriculture."
  - The logo grid includes "Homegrown By Heroes Virginia" (image `HGBH_Virginia_2022-web.jpg`, uploaded 2023-06). Its link goes to `vabeginningfarmer.alce.vt.edu/Archive/VirginiaFVC.html`, not to a VDACS "Virginia Grown" page. Whether VDACS formally co-brands this logo, and how a producer gets it, is **unverified**.
- Origin: started by the Kentucky Department of Agriculture in January 2013; FVC has run it nationally since Veterans Day 2013.

## FVC Farmer Veteran Fellowship Fund (https://farmvetco.org/fvfellowship/)
- "a small grant program that provides direct assistance to veterans who are in their beginning years of farming or ranching. The Fellowship Fund does not give money directly to the veteran, but rather to third-party vendors... Awards range from $1,000 to $5,000, and nearly $4 million has been awarded to more than 930 veterans since 2011."
- The application "opens once per year" with at least 4 weeks to apply. Panel review takes up to 2 months, awardees are notified in spring, and recipients have "six (6) months to use their award."
- Scoring criteria: farm training/experience or transferable skills, personal investment, strength of funding request, and vision/goals. "This year's Fellowship Fund cycle is now complete."
- A 2025-04 FVC story names Tractor Supply as a funder (2024 cycle).

## NCAT Armed to Farm (https://www.ncat.org/veterans-in-agriculture/armed-to-farm/, fetched 2026-10-12)
- "sustainable agriculture training for veterans and their spouses and farm partners. Using a cohort-based model ... business planning, marketing, USDA resources, and various production topics ... individualized technical assistance."
- 2026 trainings listed: Sept 28-Oct 2 (Hattiesburg, MS), Oct 5-9 (Bourbonnais, IL), Oct 28-29 (Glasgow, KY). All say "application period ... closed." **No Virginia session listed.** Director contact for hosting: armedtofarmdirector@ncat.org. ATTRA hotline: 1-800-346-9140.
