## 7 U.S.C. 2279: the statutory frame

- Definition (a)(7), "veteran farmer or rancher": served in the Armed Forces (38 U.S.C. 101(10)) and "(A) has not operated a farm or ranch; (B) has operated a farm or ranch for not more than 10 years; or (C) is a veteran ... who has first obtained status as a veteran ... during the most recent 10-year period." FSA, NRCS and VAPG all borrow this definition.
- **2501 Program**, subsection (c): grants and agreements with community-based organizations, 1890/1994 institutions, Hispanic-serving institutions, other experienced institutions of higher education, and tribes. The goal is to help socially disadvantaged and veteran farmers "in owning and operating farms and ranches" and "participating equitably in the full range of agricultural, forestry, and related programs". The statutory text includes a per-award cap of "not more than $250,000 for each year" (subsection (c), as excerpted).
- **BFRDP**, subsection (d): recipients must be "a collaborative State, Tribal, local, or regionally-based network or partnership". Terms are "not longer than 3 years" and "not more than $250,000 for each year", with a 25% cash or in-kind match (waivable). Allowed topics include "(L) agricultural rehabilitation and vocational training for veteran farmers and ranchers" and "(N) food safety and recordkeeping".
- **Funding**, subsection (l): "$50,000,000 for fiscal year 2023 and each fiscal year thereafter" in mandatory CCC money. "(A) 50 percent shall be used to carry out subsection (c); and (B) 50 percent shall be used to carry out subsection (d)."
- **Veteran set-aside**, (l)(4)(B): "Not less than 5 percent of the amounts made available to carry out subsection (d) for a fiscal year shall be used to support programs and services that address the needs of veteran farmers and ranchers." The regulation repeats this at 7 CFR 3430 (BFRDP subpart): "NIFA shall set aside not less than 5 percent of the funds to support the standard BFRDP projects ... that address the needs of veteran farmers and ranchers".
- Note: grants.gov award ceilings ($750k) exceed the "$250,000 for each year" text. Multi-year awards (3 × $250k) reconcile the two. [inferred]

## Military Veterans Agricultural Liaison (7 U.S.C. 6919)

- "The Secretary shall establish in the Department the position of Military Veterans Agricultural Liaison."
- Duties: connect returning veterans with beginning-farmer and agricultural vocational programs, including "using Federal veterans educational benefits"; give veterans program eligibility information; "serve as a resource for assisting veteran farmers and ranchers ... in applying for participation in agricultural programs"; and "advocate on behalf of veterans in interactions with employees of the Department".
- The Liaison may contract with ARS, colleges, or nonprofits for workshops, "certified vocational training", mentoring, and internships. A website is required to list GI Bill (38 U.S.C. ch. 36) approved apprenticeships and on-the-job training programs.
- The current Liaison's name and contact were not retrieved (usda.gov returned 403).

## Fit for this business [inferred]

- None of these programs give money to a for-profit custom processor. The usable angles:
  1. Use VSU's 2501 project (MD/VA/NC, through 2027) or a BFRDP-funded VA network for free business-plan, lender-readiness and FSA-application help.

  3. An AgVets nonprofit like Vet First could plausibly partner with the business as an on-farm or processing training site. That is a subcontract or partner role, not a grant to the business.
