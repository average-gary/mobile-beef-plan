# farmers.gov: Military veteran producer benefits

Retrieved 2026-10-12 with WebFetch. The page loaded, but its body was extracted by a summarizing model, so quotes are short and should be spot-checked. The page has no visible "last updated" date.

## Loans (FSA)

- Microloans: "Microloans made to Veteran farmers do not count toward the total number of years that a farmer can receive assistance through FSA's direct loan program." This matches 7 CFR 764.252(e).
- Funding preference: veterans get priority when funding is limited and applications arrive on the same date (7 CFR 764.54; part 762).
- Experience: military management experience can substitute for **one** of the three required years of farm-management experience. Note that 7 CFR 764.152(d)(1)(vi) lists "Honorable discharge" among items that substitute for up to two of the three years, in combination. The regulation governs.
- Down Payment Farm Ownership loan: partial financing to buy a family-size farm (5% down, FO rate minus 4%, floor 1.5%, per 7 CFR 764.203-.204).
- Not on the page: the 5% ML-OL rate ceiling for veteran microloans (7 CFR 764.254(a)(4)). It is regulatory, see the FSA file.

## Risk and disaster

- NAP (Non-Insured Crop Disaster Assistance): administrative (service) fees waived, plus a 50% premium reduction on buy-up coverage.
- ELAP: up to 90% cost reimbursement for livestock, honey bee and farm-raised fish losses.
- ARC/PLC: eligible for payments on farms with 10 base acres or less.
- Dairy Margin Coverage: "$100 annual administrative fee" waived.
- Crop insurance: veterans should ask agents about additional benefits. [unverified, from background knowledge] Federal crop insurance gives veteran farmers beginning-farmer-style benefits since the 2018 farm bill. The specifics were not on the page.

## Conservation (NRCS)

- EQIP and CSP: ranking priority, and "5% of funds set aside for beginning/disadvantaged producers".
- Advance payments: "at least 50% of contracted payment upfront under EQIP". This matches 7 CFR 1466 (50%-100% of material and service costs).
- Higher payment rates for conservation practices: at least 25% above standard, capped at 90% of cost, per 7 CFR 1466.

## Value-added

- VAPG: "Beginning farmer and rancher veterans receive priority ranking." In regulation this is 5 priority points (veteran or beginning, max 5), plus 5 more for qualifying group applicants. There is no veteran reserve, per 7 CFR 4284.916, 4284.924, and the scoring section.

## Fit for this business [inferred]

- The veteran owner should first register as a producer with the Frederick County FSA/NRCS service center (farm number, AD-2047, veteran self-certification). Every benefit above is triggered by the producer record.
- None of the benefits fund third-party custom slaughter. They matter if the owner runs a cattle operation alongside the rig: livestock insurance and disaster coverage, EQIP on the farm, and microloans for the herd.
