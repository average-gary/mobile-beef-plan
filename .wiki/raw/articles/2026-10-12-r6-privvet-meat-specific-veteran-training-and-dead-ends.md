# Meat-processing-specific veteran funding: what exists (as of 2026-10-12)

## NCAT Armed to Farm (training, no grant)

Source: https://www.ncat.org/veterans-in-agriculture/armed-to-farm/ (the old attra.ncat.org/armed-to-farm URL now redirects)

- "Armed to Farm provides sustainable agriculture training for veterans and their spouses and farm partners. Using a cohort-based model, we prioritize peer-to-peer learning in both classroom and on-farm settings. Participants learn about business planning, marketing, USDA resources, and various production topics."
- 2026 sessions: "September 28-October 2, 2026 Hattiesburg, Mississippi"; "October 5-9, 2026 Bourbonnais, IL"; "October 28-29, 2026 Glasgow, KY". All are marked "The application period for this training is now closed."
- Contact: armedtofarmdirector@ncat.org (Margo Hale). ATTRA hotline: 1-800-346-9140.
- The page did not show a cost. Historically the program has been free or subsidized [unverified]. It lists no grant money. NCAT does link a general "Funding Opportunities" feed.

## Veterans to Farmers (training, no grant)

Source: https://www.veteranstofarmers.org/

- Mission: "to train veterans in agricultural systems, technologies and business operations".
- Courses: Controlled Environment Agriculture (hydroponics/aquaponics), Market Farming, Urban Farming Series, Business Planning Course. "Free For Veterans!"
- Location: Yampa, CO. A 501(c)(3). Mentions "American AgCredit Grants" without details. No livestock or meat content.

## The Butcher's Guild: DOMAIN SQUATTED

- https://www.thebutchersguild.org/ redirects to thebutchersguild.org and serves an Indonesian-language page ("Tentang Kami thebutchersguild adalah destinasi online ..."). The original butcher professional network is no longer at this domain. Do not treat any "Butcher's Guild veteran scholarship" listing as live.

## AAMP / American Meat Processors Foundation: NOT VERIFIED

- aamp.com and aamp.com/scholarships return a Cloudflare "Just a moment..." challenge (403) to automated fetches. We could not confirm scholarship amounts, eligibility, or any veteran preference [unverified]. Check manually in a browser.

## Meat Institute / Meat Foundation

- meatinstitute.org links "Meat Foundation ( Scholarships )". We did not follow the link. These are [inferred] academic scholarships for students, not startup grants.

## Fit notes [inferred]

- No veteran-and-meat-processing grant was found. Listicles that claim one should be checked against a primary page before anyone relies on them.
- Free veteran training (IVMF EBV, Warrior Rising Academy/University, Armed to Farm) helps produce the business plan that FVC, Warrior Rising Showers, and the USDA and state programs covered in other rounds all require.
- For hands-on butchery skills, the earlier rounds' sources (Penn State butcher apprenticeship, RAPPCE butchery program in raw/articles) remain the relevant leads. Veteran status may unlock GI Bill coverage for registered apprenticeships [unverified; check VA approval of the specific program].
