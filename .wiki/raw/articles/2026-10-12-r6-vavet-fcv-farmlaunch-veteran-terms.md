# Farm Credit of the Virginias: FarmLaunch veteran terms

Fetched live 2026-10-12 (page copyright 2026). Note: `fcvirginias.com` has a TLS mismatch. The working domain is `farmcreditofvirginias.com`.

## FarmLaunch page (verbatim)
- "FarmLaunch—a program designed to support young and beginning farmers, including military veterans, who are entering the agricultural industry with limited resources."
- "FarmLaunch is available for the purchase of farm-related assets, including real estate, machinery and livestock, as well as farm operating expenses. Beyond financing, the program connects participants with educational resources and mentorship..."
- "Additional Benefits of FarmLaunch include: Flexible underwriting standards and customizable loan terms; Loan origination fee discount (up to $500); Collaboration with USDA Farm Service Agency Guaranteed Farm Loan Programs; Patronage refund eligibility"
- A testimonial mentions the "AgBiz Planner program" and a "YBS farmer advisory committee". The AgBiz Planner page returned 404, so its terms are unverified.

## What the page does NOT state (as of 2026-10-12)
- No veteran-specific rate reduction, grant, set-aside, or extra fee waiver.
- No published definition of "beginning" (Farm Credit System YBS rules generally use 10 years or fewer of farming and under age 36 for "young". These are not stated on this page and are unverified for FCV's FarmLaunch).
- No loan size cap.

## Interest rates and terms page (https://www.farmcreditofvirginias.com/loans/interest-rates-terms)
- Fixed, variable, prime-based variable, and fixed-then-variable (balloon/adjustable 1/3/5-year) options.
- "The length of a loan repayment can range from one month to 30 years."
- Intermediate-term (1-10 years): "Machinery and equipment purchases"; long-term: "amortization terms up to 20 years, or even 30 years if the loan is related to the borrower's primary home", including "facilities construction".
- No rates are published.

## YBS Knowledge Center (https://www.farmcreditofvirginias.com/knowledge-center/young-beginning-and-small-farmers)
- "resources specific to the needs of young, beginning, small, minority and veteran farmers." Contact KnowledgeCenter@FCVirginias.com.
- Veteran links are referrals only: WVDA Veterans & Heroes to Agriculture, Operation Welcome Home VAT program (WV), Arcadia Veteran Farmer Program, Farmer Veteran Coalition / Homegrown By Heroes / Farmer Veteran Fellowship Fund, NCAT Armed to Farm, Heroes to Hives, AgrAbility, Farm Aid.
- FCV also runs an ACE (Agricultural Career Enhancement) Grant Program and a CCF (Charitable Contributions Fund) grant program. Their terms were not fetched; CCF is likely for nonprofits [unverified].

## Fit notes [inferred]
- A mobile unit (truck/trailer plus equipment) fits an intermediate 1-10 year equipment loan, and a cooler building fits long-term facilities construction.
- Pairing with an FSA guarantee is the main lever for a thin-collateral startup.
- Veteran status adds no published pricing benefit at FCV beyond the FarmLaunch fee discount.
