# Virginia SBDC veteran services and Laurel Ridge SBDC

## Virginia SBDC Network veterans page (https://www.virginiasbdc.org/programs/veterans/, fetched 2026-10-12)
- "As a result of the Federal mandate of the Veterans Entrepreneurship and Small Business Development Act of 1999, Public Law 106-50 ... Virginia SBDC includes Virginia veterans as a target group for special support in providing technical business assistance to small businesses throughout Virginia."
- The only named program is the **Virginia Small Business Veteran of the Year Award**: "a specially designed and crafter plaque", with the name inscribed on a plaque displayed at the Virginia War Memorial in Richmond. No cash prize is mentioned.
- Eligibility: "Currently owns at least 51% of a Virginia-based for-profit business. OR Has owned at least 51% of a for-profit business for at least one year in the past"; must live in Virginia; no past winners; DD214 required.
- "The nomination and application window is June – September." Winner announced in early November. Judging criteria: advocacy via veteran organizations, advocacy via community organizations, business leadership and service.
- Contact: Tom Tanner, ttanner@roanokesmallbusiness.org.
- Recent winners: 2025 Hillary Boyce (BAFO Services), 2024 Pamela Lucas.

## Laurel Ridge SBDC (https://laurelridgesbdc.org/, fetched 2026-10-12)
- Address line: "Middletown, VA 22645" (Laurel Ridge Community College, formerly Lord Fairfax). `lfsbdc.org` redirects here. Hosted with GMU as the regional lead, per the gmu.edu link.
- The "Veterans Program" menu item links to `https://www.sba.gov/starting-business/how-start-business/business-types/veteran-owned-businesses`. **No local veteran-specific program** is listed.
- Appointment intake: clients.virginiasbdc.org (center=46070).
- **RISE (Regional Initiative Supporting Entrepreneurship)**: a pitch competition with cash awards. The pages last mention 2016-17 and 2017 winners, and the site still shows a 2020 holiday banner, so it is likely dormant [inferred]. Rules: compete only if "in business for at least two years", "annual revenues not exceeding $5 million", EIN, located in "Frederick County, City of Winchester, Clarke County, Warren County, Town of Front Royal, Shenandoah County or Page County", $99 non-refundable fee, five 120-minute Entrepreneur Cafe classes, and a specific cash-award request.
- "Banking/Lending" page lists United Bank, Business Finance Group (BFG, an SBA 504 CDC), and VSBFA. These are referrals only.

## Fit notes [inferred]
- Use Laurel Ridge SBDC for free counseling: projections, lender package, and the VSBFA microloan or Capital Connect application (already covered elsewhere).
- RISE would not fit a startup even if revived (it requires 2 years in business).
- The veteran award brings no capital. At most it is minor PR after 1+ years of operation.
