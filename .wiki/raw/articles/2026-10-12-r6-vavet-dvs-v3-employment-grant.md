# V3 Employment Grant (Virginia DVS)

**Access note:** dvs.virginia.gov returns HTTP 403 to automated fetches. This content comes from the Wayback Machine capture of 2024-11-03 (`web.archive.org/web/20241103025427/...v3-employment-grant`). The 2025-2026 page could not be retrieved because the Internet Archive went offline mid-session. The live V3 employer portal (v3.dvs.virginia.gov, an Angular app) still has a "Grants Application" dashboard in its JS bundle (fetched 2026-10-12), so the grant appears to still exist. Treat the dollar amounts as 2024 terms and verify them for FY27.

## Amounts
- "Companies may qualify for up to $10,000 in grants per year, with $1,000 being awarded per eligible Veteran hired."
- "In 2015, the General Assembly unanimously appropriated $100,000 for the purposes of establishing an employment grant for V3-certified employers."
- "The V3 Program has expended all available grant funds for FY24. All approved grants will be dispersed starting the FY25 fiscal year (beginning July 1, 2024). Employers will still be eligible to apply for up to $10,000 in the FY25."

## Employer eligibility
- "A V3-Certified Company with an Employer ID#"
- "Fewer than 300 employees under common control, at time of grant application"
- "A Business to qualify (no public agencies or non-profits)"
- No repeat submissions for the same veteran in any year.

## Veteran-hire eligibility (page bullets)
- "Be employed within 5 years of discharge from active duty"
- "Hired in a full-time capacity"
- "Hired and retained for at least one year — on or after July 1, 2014"

## Statutory language quoted on page (conflicts with bullets)
> "Such funds shall be used to provide grants beginning July 1, 2015, to any business located in Virginia with 300 or fewer employees which has hired a veteran on or after July 1, 2014, with the following additional requirements: (a) each such veteran shall have been hired within one year of the date of his or her discharge from active military service; (b) each such veteran shall have been continuously employed by the business in a full-time job for at least one year; and (c) each such veteran shall have been paid at least the prevailing average wage of the jurisdiction in which the job is located."

- Discrepancy: the bullets say within **5 years** of discharge, the quoted law says within **1 year**. The language appears to come from the Appropriation Act, not the Code (it is not in Title 2.2, Chapter 20, checked 2026-10-12). Confirm the current window with a V3 Employer Liaison.

## Related DVS programs (from the 2024 Veteran Entrepreneurship page capture)
- The DVS "Veteran Entrepreneurship" page (web.archive.org/web/20240716132908/.../education-employment/veteran-entrepreneurship) is a referral page only. It lists no state grant or loan. Contact: v3transition@dvs.virginia.gov. It links out to USDA veterans resources, the IVMF EBV (Entrepreneurship Bootcamp for Veterans), Hire Our Heroes, and SBA veteran-owned business pages.
- The V3 Transition Program, MEWI, VVFS (peer/family support), and the Military Spouse Liaison (Va. Code § 2.2-2002.2) are not business-funding programs.

## Fit notes [inferred]
- For this startup: get V3 certification first (it is free; it involves HR training and a hiring-goal commitment, terms unverified). Then, if a recently separated veteran is hired full-time as a cutter or kill-floor hand and kept 12 months at or above the local prevailing wage, file for $1,000.
- The owner's own veteran status gets nothing from V3. It is an employer hiring incentive, not owner support.
- Seasonal or part-time butchering staff would not qualify (full-time is required).
