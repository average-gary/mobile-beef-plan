# Reimbursement-grant freeze, 2025 (NMPAN)

The article says it is opinion: "The views expressed in this article are those of Rebecca Thistlethwaite's and not those of Oregon State University where NMPAN is housed."

## Extracted facts

- "many federal grant programs have been frozen due to President Trump's executive orders. This means they are not making payments or reimbursements on millions of dollars of grants."
- Payments stalled despite TROs: "Even though two judges have issued temporary restraining orders directing the federal government to continue funding their existing grant contracts, many government departments and agencies are still not processing payments after January 19th, 2025."
- Programs named: MPIRG (2 rounds), MPEPP [sic, MPPEP] (2 rounds), Local MCap, the Indigenous Animals Harvesting and Meat Processing Grant Program (IAG), plus VAPG, LFPP, REAP, and NRCS grants, which were "effectively frozen, despite the judge's orders."
- Reported cases (names withheld):
  - "$317,645 frozen for a vertically integrated pastured poultry operator for processing equipment, H2A program, and farm water system upgrades"
  - "$100,000 in refrigeration upgrades frozen for a small custom-exempt operator who was going to go under USDA inspection but now will not be able to"
  - "$120,000 for a new farmer-owned processing facility to upgrade their humane handling equipment"
  - "$50,000 to close out a MPIRG grant and finish paying their suppliers for new packaging equipment"
- AAMP quote: "Companies have poured concrete for new construction and ordered equipment for expanded processing. When the payment is frozen, or worse, canceled, the consequences could drive a small processor out of business."

## Unverified / follow-up

- How the freeze was resolved (whether these specific grants were later paid or terminated) is **not** in this article. It was not verified this round.

## Fit for this business [inferred]

- This is the cash-flow trap in concrete form. Under a reimbursement grant the owner pays vendors first. A freeze, a documentation dispute, or a slow 30-day payment cycle leaves the owner holding vendor debt.
- Rule for the plan: count no grant dollar in the startup capital stack until it is received. Size bridge financing, such as a line of credit, to the full reimbursable amount, not just the match.
- A custom-exempt operator was in the affected list. Grant exposure applies even at this business's regulatory tier.
