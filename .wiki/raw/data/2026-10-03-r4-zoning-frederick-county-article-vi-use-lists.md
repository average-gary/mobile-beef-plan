# Frederick County Ch. 165 Article VI: use lists that matter for a cooler or cutting dock

**Retrieval.** The ecode360 parts were read through Wayback Machine 2026 snapshots (`https://web.archive.org/web/2026/https://ecode360.com/<ID>`). The page header says "Includes legislation through 01-08-2025". The allowed-use sections of B2, B3, TM, and M1 carry "[Amended 7-10-2024]". The M2 allowed-use section has no separate amendment note; the M2 part header lists amendments 7-9-1997 through 12-13-2023. Every list below is quoted verbatim. Each list is filtered to the entries that bear on this plan; uses that are not relevant were left out. "Not listed" means a full read of the district's allowed and conditional lists found no such use. This is not legal advice. Use classification belongs to the Zoning Administrator.

## B2 General Business District (Part 603; ecode360 13804443)

**Intent (§ 165-603.01), excerpt:** "The intent of this district is to provide large areas for a variety of business, office and service uses. ... Businesses allowed involve frequent and direct access by the general public but not heavy truck traffic on a constant basis other than that required for delivery of retail goods. ... Nuisance factors are to be avoided. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) may be used to assist the Zoning Administrator in classifying the permitted uses."

**Allowed uses (§ 165-603.02), relevant entries verbatim:**
- "Food and Beverage Retailers, excluding Fruit and Vegetable Stands"
- "Food services, Restaurants and Other Eating Places"
- "Self-Service Storage Facilities"
- "Consumer Goods Rental excluding Lawn and Garden equipment"
- "Technical and Trade Schools"
- "Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services, excluding the following: Outdoor Display Advertising, Research and Development Services, and Test Laboratories including General Business Offices"
- "Veterinary services with all activities and animals kept within the fully enclosed primary structure, excluding livestock"

**Not listed:** food manufacturing, animal slaughtering or processing, rendering, warehousing and storage, refrigerated storage, truck transportation, trailer rental.
**Conditional uses:** B2 has no conditional-use section (the part has only §§ 603.01 and 603.02).

## B3 Industrial Transition District (Part 604; ecode360 13804447)

**Intent (§ 165-604.01), excerpt:** "The intent of this district is to provide for heavy commercial activities, involving larger scale marketing or wholesaling, in locations that are separate from but in the vicinity of business and industrial areas. ... In these areas, there will be a mixture of automobile and truck traffic. ... It is intended that the uses in this district shall not be sources of noise, dust, smoke or other nuisances. ... The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) may be used to assist the Zoning Administrator in classifying the permitted uses."

**Allowed uses (§ 165-604.02), relevant entries verbatim:**
- "Warehousing and Storage"
- "Truck Transportation and related support activities"
- "Wholesale Trade Businesses excluding livestock, farm products, mining and chemical storage"
- "Food and Beverage Retailers"
- "Food Services, Restaurants and Other Eating Places"
- "Self-Service Storage Facilities"
- "Consumer Goods Rentals"
- "Car, Truck, Utility Trailers, and RV (Recreational Vehicle) Rental and Leasing"
- "General Automotive, Motorcycle, and Truck Repair, Services and Parking"
- "Vocational Schools (Technical, Trade, Business, Computer and Management Training)"
- "Accessory Retailing"
- "Flex-Tech"

**Conditional uses (§ 165-604.03), complete list:** "Tractor Truck and Tractor Truck Trailer Parking"

**Not listed:** food manufacturing, animal slaughtering or processing, rendering. Note that the wholesale entry excludes "farm products".

## TM Technology-Manufacturing Park District (Part 605; ecode360 13804500)

**Intent (§ 165-605.01), excerpt:** "The TM District is designed to provide areas for Economic Development Authority (EDA) targeted industries, data centers, offices, low-impact industrial, assembly, and manufacturing uses. ... This district shall be located in a business campus-like setting ... The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) shall be used to assist the Zoning Administrator in classifying the permitted uses."

**Permitted uses (§ 165-605.02), relevant entries verbatim:**
- "Food, Beverage, and Tobacco Manufacturing excluding Seafood Product Preparation and Packaging and Animal Slaughtering and Processing"
- "Technical and Trade Schools"
- "Flex-Tech"

**Secondary or accessory uses (§ 165-605.03), verbatim:** permitted "only in conjunction with and secondary to a permitted principal use": "Warehousing/Distribution up to 50% gross square footage (or up to 75% gross square footage where served directly by rail)"

**Design requirements (§ 165-605.04), relevant:** "No TM District rezoning shall be approved for less than 10 contiguous acres." "Loading docks or loading entrances shall be completely screened from view from public streets and adjoining properties by a six-foot-tall opaque fencing, masonry walls, berms or a double row of evergreen tree plantings."

**Not listed:** standalone warehousing or storage, truck transportation, trailer rental. TM has no conditional-use section.

**Intent (§ 165-606.01), excerpt:** "The intent of this district is to provide for a variety of light manufacturing, commercial office and heavy commercial uses in well-planned industrial settings. Uses are allowed which do not create noise, smoke, dust or other hazards. Uses are allowed which do not adversely affect nearby residential or business areas. ... The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) shall be used to assist the Zoning Administrator in classifying the permitted uses."

**Allowed uses (§ 165-606.02), relevant entries verbatim:**
- "Food, Beverage, and Tobacco Manufacturing excluding Seafood Product Preparation and Packaging and Animal Slaughtering and Processing"
- "Truck Transportation"
- "Wholesale Trade"
- "Self-Service Storage Facilities"
- "Truck, Utility Trailer, and RV (Recreational Vehicle) Rental and Leasing"
- "Truck or Fleet Maintenance Facilities"
- "Machinery Cleaning"
- "Solid Waste Collection, Materials Recovery Facilities, Septic Tank and Related Services, and Other Miscellaneous Management Services"
- "Food Services, Restaurants, and Other Eating Places"
- "Technical and Trade Schools"

**Conditional uses (§ 165-606.03), complete list:** "Tractor Truck and Tractor Truck Trailer Parking"; "Commercial Recreation, Indoors (as defined), including Fitness and Recreational Sports Centers"

**Not listed:** "Warehousing and Storage" as a named use (it is listed in B3 but not in M1), animal slaughtering and processing (expressly excluded), rendering.

## M2 Industrial General District (Part 607; ecode360 13804515)

**Intent (§ 165-607.01), verbatim:** "The intent of this district is to provide for a wide variety of heavy manufacturing, office and heavy traffic uses, including those which may not be compatible with nearby residential and general business areas. Performance controls are used to control potential nuisances, especially in relation to zoning district boundaries. Such industrial areas shall be provided with safe and sufficient access. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) shall be used to assist the Zoning Administrator in classifying the permitted uses."

**Allowed uses (§ 165-607.02), relevant entries verbatim:**
- "All Uses Allowed in the M1 (Light Industrial) Zoning District"
- "Food Manufacturing including Animal Slaughtering and Processing, Seafood Product Preparation and Packaging, and Rendering and Meat Byproduct Processing"
- "Waste Management and Remediation Services including incinerators"
- "Tractor Truck and Tractor Truck Trailer Parking as primary use"

**Conditional uses:** M2 has no conditional-use section (the part has only §§ 607.01 and 607.02).

## NAICS classification language

- B2 and B3: NAICS "**may** be used to assist the Zoning Administrator in classifying the permitted uses."
- TM, M1, and M2: NAICS "**shall** be used to assist the Zoning Administrator in classifying the permitted uses."
- The use names follow NAICS headings: "Animal Slaughtering and Processing" (NAICS 3116), "Rendering and Meat Byproduct Processing" (311613), "Warehousing and Storage" (4931), "Truck Transportation" (484). ## Application to the options [inferred]

All of this section is [inferred] from the quoted text. None of it is a Zoning Administrator determination.

1. **Cooler plus cutting dock (Option B: the unit cuts and packs while docked at the cooler).** Cutting carcasses into retail cuts is NAICS 311612 Meat Processed from Carcasses, which falls under "Animal Slaughtering and Processing" (NAICS 3116). M1 and TM expressly exclude that use, and B2 and B3 do not list it. The only by-right district is M2. No business or industrial district offers it as a conditional use, so outside M2 the path is a rezoning to M2. In RA the path is CUP BB "Slaughterhouses" (see the RA source). § 165-204.17A is worded to reach "any ... establishment where animals or fowl, dead or alive, are processed" without naming a district. So its setbacks, under-roof, and screening rules may also apply on an M2 parcel; this is unverified. This corrects the wiki's earlier statement that "a commercial parcel with public sewer may be simpler": B2 (General Business) does not allow the use.
2. **Cooler for storage only (Options C and D: no cutting on site).** B3 is the clean fit: "Warehousing and Storage" by right. M1 does not name warehousing. A carcass cooler there would need the Zoning Administrator to classify it under a listed use, such as "Wholesale Trade" (doubtful, because the operator stores product it does not own) or as similar to a listed use. Treat M1 as unconfirmed, not as "probably allowed". M2 inherits M1's list, so the same gap applies there, unless the cooler is accessory to an M2 "Animal Slaughtering and Processing" use. TM allows warehousing only as a secondary use under a permitted principal use, on a site of at least 10 acres. In B3 the "Wholesale Trade Businesses" entry excludes "farm products", but that limits wholesaling, not "Warehousing and Storage". Whether hanging an owner's uninspected carcass for a fee counts as refrigerated warehousing (NAICS 493120) is a classification question for the Zoning Administrator.
3. **Rental and storage (Option D: owners rent the mobile unit; the company stores their carcasses).** Renting out the trailer fits "Car, Truck, Utility Trailers, and RV (Recreational Vehicle) Rental and Leasing" (B3) and "Truck, Utility Trailer, and RV ... Rental and Leasing" (M1, so also M2). A 53 ft semi rig is not a utility trailer, so the classification is uncertain. A nearer NAICS fit may be 532490, Other Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment Rental, which no district lists by name. B3 combines "Warehousing and Storage", trailer rental, "General Automotive ... Truck Repair, Services and Parking", and (by CUP) tractor-truck parking. That makes B3 the single district that best covers a storage-plus-rental yard with no processing. Washing the unit between renters on site may be a "Machinery Cleaning" use (listed in M1 only). Wash-water disposal is a separate utility question (see the Frederick Water source).
4. **Education add-on (Option E).** "Technical and Trade Schools" (B2, TM, M1, and M2 via M1) and "Vocational Schools (Technical, Trade, Business, Computer and Management Training)" (B3) are by right. A classroom butchery course at a B3 or M1 site looks like a permitted use. Hands-on cutting of carcasses at that site probably brings back the "Animal Slaughtering and Processing" question (M2 only). Courses held on the renter's own farm are outside these districts entirely.
5. **Not changed by this source:** whether a mobile unit killing on RA farms is a "slaughterhouse" use, VDACS permit status, cutting labor, and blood and wash-water handling on farms.
