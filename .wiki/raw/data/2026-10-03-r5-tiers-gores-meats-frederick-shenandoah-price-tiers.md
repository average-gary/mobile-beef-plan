# Gore's Meats processing charges (Stephens City and Edinburg, VA)

Sources:
- https://goresmeats.com/stephens-city-processing/ (web page, fetched 2026-10-03, footer "(c) Copyright. 2026. Gore's Processing, Inc.")
- https://goresmeats.com/edinburg-foltz-processing/ (web page, fetched 2026-10-03)
- https://goresmeats.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/SC-Processing-Charges-1.1.23.pdf ("As of Jan 1., 2023")
- https://goresmeats.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/ED-Processing-Charges-1.1.23.pdf ("As of Jan 1, 2023")

Gore's is a family business that says it has served the Shenandoah Valley "for over 50 years". It has a retail shop, Gore's Fresh Meats (Stephens City). It also runs a wholesale route "from Charlestown WV to Waynesboro VA" and sells freezer packs of its own beef: about $900 per quarter, $1,800 per half and $3,600 per whole, which it calls "ROUGH ESTIMATES". **This is the incumbent processor in the plan's home county (Frederick).** Earlier rounds (r2 capacity file) had found no Gore's price list. These pages close that gap.

## Plant labels (as stated on site)
- Stephens City: "State inspected, not for re-sale". Phone 540-869-1029.
- Edinburg Foltz: "Federally inspected, for re-sale". Phone 540-984-8138.
- [inferred] "State inspected, not for re-sale" probably means custom-exempt service run in a plant that also holds state inspection. The site does not explain it.

## Current beef pricing (web pages, fetched 2026-10-03)

**Stephens City (custom, not for sale)**
- Beef slaughter fee: $100.00
- Vacuum sealed: $1.05/lb hanging weight "to custom cut, vacuum seal, label, quick freeze"
- Beef over 30 months: +$50.00. The spinal column is removed, so there is no NY strip or porterhouse option.
- "Dry-Age Beef over 21 days: discuss at booking"
- Storage: "We will hold meat in our freezer space with no charge for 3 weeks, but if you haven't picked it up by then a $50 charge will be incurred per week."
- Pork slaughter $90 + $1.05/lb. Scald hog kill $90; hogs over 300 lb are +$0.10/lb over 300. Lamb is a $115 flat fee.

**Edinburg (custom, not for sale)**
- Appointment deposit $100, applied to the butcher fee. "Butchering & Rendering Fee" $100.
- Vacuum sealed OR paper wrap: $1.05/lb hanging weight
- Over 30 months: +$50. Dry age over 21 days: discuss at booking.
- Storage: free for 1 week, then $50 per month.

**Edinburg (resale, weigh and label)**
- Beef slaughter $100 + rendering $20
- Beef cuts, vac seal, weigh and label: $1.25/lb hanging weight ("private label")
- Ground beef vac seal, weigh and label: +$0.65/lb
- Over 30 months +$50
- Pork $90 slaughter + $1.15/lb; 1 chop/pack $10/whole; sausage patties, links and stuffed $0.75/lb
- Lamb/goat weigh and label: $135/head flat
- Storage: free for 1 month, then $40 per month.

## Price movement 2023 to 2026 (from the two snapshots)
- Beef kill fee: $75 to $100 (+33%).
- Custom cut and wrap: $0.95 to $1.05/lb (+10.5%).
- Resale weigh and label: $1.15 to $1.25/lb (+8.7%).
- [inferred] The current pages do not list the kill-and-age ($105) or primals ($0.70/lb) tiers. They may have been dropped or simply not posted on the web page.

## Relevance to tiered mobile pricing [inferred]
- A Frederick County kill-and-chill tier already exists as a benchmark: $105 in 2023 at a fixed plant, with the hauling done by the farmer. A mobile kill-only fee has to be judged against that number plus the farmer's trucking cost and time.
- Gore's deliberately prices one per-pound rate for vac and paper wrap, with no wrap-type tier. Upcharges sit on labor-heavy options instead: single-steak packs, patties, cubing, slicing, weigh and label.
- Its storage charges work as a penalty for late pickup, not as a revenue line. The free window is 1-4 weeks, followed by $40-50 per week or per month.
