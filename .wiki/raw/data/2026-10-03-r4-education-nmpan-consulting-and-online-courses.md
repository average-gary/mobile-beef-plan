# NMPAN technical assistance, consulting and online-course pricing

Fetched pages: nichemeatprocessing.org/technical-assistance/, meatprocessoracademy.com (home, /faq, /mastermind-program), westernmeatschool.com (home, /faq). Note: nichemeatprocessing.org/consultants/ returns 404. The old "consultant directory" seems to have been replaced by the technical-assistance page [inferred].

## Free consulting (the "free" competitor tier)

**NMPAN Staff Office Hours.** Free 30-minute calls with Rebecca Thistlethwaite (Director) and David Zarling (Program Manager).

**Meat & Poultry Processing Technical Assistance Consultants.** NMPAN describes them as "Experts on different aspects of the meat supply chain & processing ... Book a free call!" They are named with their specialties:
- Brian Brozovic: marketing and sales, working with wholesalers and distributors, operations, compliance programs
- Tyler Cook: new product development, value-added and RTE products, HACCP
- Dan DeHaan: facility design, engineering, wastewater systems
- Samantha Egolf: feasibility studies, business planning, pre-construction planning
- Chris Fuller: processing operations, facility design and equipment, HACCP (also the author of the PEC 2021 Virginia study already in the wiki)
- Greg Gunthorp: on-farm USDA processing, poultry, red meat and poultry in the same facility
- Elaine Lemmon: inventory, financial statements, budgeting and cash flow
- Trevor Morones: food safety management systems, audit prep, HACCP certification
- Joe M. Regenstein PhD: religious slaughter, halal/kosher, animal welfare
- **Tracy Smaciarz: "Custom Exempt operations; Transitioning from custom to inspected; Mobile Processing in real life"** (this is the closest match to this plan)
- Arion Thiboumery: scaling, product costing, efficiencies and throughput
- Abbe Turner: grant proposals, ag business plans and value-added products
- Good Meat Project consultants for marketing (linked at goodmeatproject.org/nmpan-office-hours)

**Peer 2 Peer Consulting Program.** "Up to 10 hours of free coaching/consulting will be provided to those who are selected." It is open to "niche meat producers, processors, and meat brands" and can cover some travel. "Program is open in 2025 for up to 20 projects." It is funded by the Northwest & Rocky Mountain Regional Food Business Center and the USDA Meat & Poultry Processing Technical Assistance program. Applicants must apply, and "not all requests will be fulfilled."

## Paid online courses (price anchors)

| Course | Audience | Content | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Western Meat School | Farmers, ranchers, butchers who direct-market meat | 8 modules, "over 20 hours of recorded presentations", 6-month access, certificate if you write a graded 2-page business plan | **$150 per person or family/business** (one shared login); half-off scholarship ($75 value) for BIPOC and hardship applicants |
| Meat Processor Academy (NMPAN + AAMP + Kitchen Table Consultants) | Processors with 25-150 employees, 2+ years in operation; custom-exempt plants that plan to go inspected are welcome | 12 core + 2 optional modules, 14 hours, 6 months of access and instructor email | **$150 for up to 2 people per business**. FAQ: "A course with this level of content and speakers would normally cost $500-1,000." Subsidized by USDA AMS |
| MPA Mastermind | Up to 15 similarly sized processors | Feb 2025 to Mar 2026. Monthly 3-hour online sessions (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, ratios, strategy, risk, margins, "Setting Service Fees", labor, hiring, inventory) | **$1,500**, with $500 refunded on completion. Includes "10 FREE Hours of 1-on-1 Financial Coaching" described as "a $1,500 value alone" |

Western Meat School says it is "not geared towards those who want to start new meat processing facilities". Start-ups are told MPA content "may be advanced for you."

## Relevance to an advise/educate-renters offering [inferred]

- The going rate for structured *online* meat-business education is about $150 per person or business, and that price is grant-subsidized.
- MPA implies an unsubsidized value of $500-1,000 for a 14-hour course and about $150/hour for 1-on-1 coaching.
- Short consultations (30 minutes) and up to 10 hours of project consulting are free through NMPAN. A paid consulting offer for small producers or would-be processors in Virginia would need to offer something local and hands-on that these remote, national programs do not.
- Tracy Smaciarz (custom-exempt and mobile) is a free benchmark expert and a possible mentor.
