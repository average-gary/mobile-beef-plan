# Insureon — Consulting business insurance cost

**Methodology (per the page):** "Our figures are sourced from the median cost of policies purchased by consulting businesses through Insureon. The median offers a better estimate of what your business is likely to pay because it excludes outlier high and low premiums." The page shows dateModified 2026-06-30. Insureon is a commercial broker with an interest in selling these policies, so credibility is 3. The data is a sample of its own customers, not an actuarial survey.

## Median monthly premiums, consulting businesses

| Policy | Median premium | Annual range | Typical limits / deductible |
|---|---|---|---|
| General liability | $32/mo | ~$250 to >$1,400 | $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate; $500 deductible (83% choose these limits) |
| Professional liability (E&O) | $62/mo | ~$400 to >$3,750 | $1M / $1M; $1,000 deductible (71% choose these limits) |
| Business owner's policy (GL + property) | $61/mo | ~$375 to >$3,500 | - |
| Workers' compensation | $44/mo | ~$250 to >$3,000 | - |
| Cyber | $81/mo | - | - |
| Commercial auto | $231/mo | - | - |
| Fidelity bonds | $104/mo | - | - |

## Distribution notes

- GL: "47% pay less than $30 per month and 39% pay between $30 and $60 each month."
- E&O: "38% pay less than $50 per month and 34% pay between $50 and $100 per month."
- Cost drivers named: size of business, how often you interact with clients, revenue, premises size, industry risk, years in operation, claims history, location. The page's example: "a cybersecurity consulting firm will likely pay more for this policy than an education consultant who works from their home."

## Coverage definitions as stated

- GL covers a client's bodily injury (for example a trip and fall in your office), damage to client property, and "advertising injuries" (slander, libel, copyright infringement).
- Professional liability / E&O covers "legal fees related to accusations of professional negligence." Example: "if a research consultant makes a recommendation that leads to financial losses for a client."
- Workers' comp is "typically" required for employees and recommended for sole proprietors. Most policies include employer's liability.

## Application to the advise/consult/educate option [inferred]

- A one-person Virginia advisory business would face E&O claims like these: a plan for an MSU, cooler, or custom-exempt setup that leads to a regulatory violation or failed inspection; a feasibility estimate a client relied on; or advice on carcass handling that led to spoilage. E&O at about $750/yr median ($62 x 12) plus GL at about $384/yr gives roughly **$1,100-$1,200/yr** before any workshop or rental exposure.
- **Hands-on classes with knives, live animals, or firearms** are *not* the "education consultant who works from home" risk class. Expect higher GL rating, or exclusions for firearms, animals, and participant injury that require special-event or instructor coverage. No published premium for that class was found in this source.
- **Equipment rental to the public** is a different underwriting class: rental-equipment GL plus inland marine for the trailers. Consulting BOP/GL rates should not be used as a proxy for it.
- Benchmarks are national medians. Virginia-specific quotes and ag-specialist carriers (farm-bureau programs) were not checked here.
