# NC Cooperative Extension MPU rental programs (2026 fee sheets)

Sources (all fetched 2026-10-03):
- Stokes: https://stokes.ces.ncsu.edu/rental-equipment/mobile-poultry-processing-unit/ (updated Aug 24, 2026)
- Orange/Durham: https://orange.ces.ncsu.edu/mobile-poultry-processing-units-orange/ (updated Jun 30, 2026)
- Rowan: https://rowan.ces.ncsu.edu/animal-agriculture/rental-equipment-2/poultry-mobile-processing-unit-mpu/ (updated Sep 22, 2026)

Other NC county units cross-linked from these pages: Caswell, Randolph, Harnett, Jones, Watauga. Directories: MeatSuite processor directory (filter "MPU") and a CEFS map of MPUs for rent in NC.

## Fee table (2026)

| Program | Rental fee | Payment | Deposit / penalty | Cleaning | Training |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stokes County | $75.00 for rentals up to 4 days | not stated | $25 deposit required for future reservations if renter no-shows/fails to cancel | not stated on page (User Agreement linked, view-only) | Required: "All renters must attend a processing training hosted by N.C. Cooperative Extension prior to renting the unit for the first time." Trainings late spring and late fall; none in Stokes in Fall 2026 |
| Orange + Durham (joint) | $50.00 per 3-day; $75.00 per 5-day | Online credit card only; no check or cash | not stated | "a cleaning charge will be assessed if the unit is not properly cleaned and the user will be required to participate in additional training prior to the next rental" (amount not stated) | Required before first rental; covers animal welfare, biosecurity, pest control, equipment, sanitation, food safety, start-to-finish processing; offered at least twice/year |
| Rowan County | $25.00/day, 3-day minimum = $75.00 | Exact cash or check only | $25 deposit after no-show/failure to cancel | $100 fee if returned not thoroughly cleaned; office suggests keeping unit an extra day to clean | Not stated as required; agent assistance available by appointment |

## Eligibility and scheduling
- Stokes: open to anyone who completed training, first-come first-served, not limited to county residents; office "reserves the right to direct producers to MPUs located closer to them." Reservations typically taken six months ahead.
- Orange/Durham: renters "must be located in either Durham or Orange counties." 3-day windows Tue-Thu or Fri-Mon; 5-day Fri-Wed. Office closed weekends. User Agreement signed at pickup.
- Rowan: anyone, first-come first-served; same right to redirect.

## Who tows / what the renter supplies
- Units housed in a 5' x 8' enclosed towable trailer (Orange, Rowan). Renter brings a vehicle with a 2-inch ball and "working connectors for trailer lights and brakes" (Orange, Rowan). I.e., the renter tows; no delivery service is offered on any of the three pages.
- Renter supplies own propane (20-lb tanks compatible), plus (Stokes) a bucket for antimicrobial dip and coolers.

## Equipment included (Stokes example)
8 large poultry cones, 4 XL turkey cones, cone stand, Featherman scalder/dunker with shackle, Featherman Pro plucker, 2 stainless tables, NSF hose, hand truck, trash can, feather crates, knives ("use at your own risk"), trailer lock, user manual. Orange adds a 110v-220v transformer. Rowan lists Featherman scalder, plucker, Roto-Dunker, 10 cones, 2 folding tables, 2 hoses.

## Operating tips published
Process on a slope for drainage; keep near power to avoid long drop cords; scalder should not exceed 145 F at start, cools to about 130 F during use (Rowan); set up left to right: cones, scalder, plucker, evisceration tables.

## Relevance to a beef venture [inferred]
These are pure equipment-rental (renter does the slaughter under the PL 90-492-type producer exemption for poultry). The pricing ($25/day, ~$75 per rental) is nominal and subsidized by Extension; it is a benchmark for training-gated rental structure (mandatory training, user agreement, cleaning fee, no-show deposit), not for commercial rental pricing.
